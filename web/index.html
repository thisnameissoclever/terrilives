How to play

  1. Select a person to see their needs and life satisfaction.
  2. Tap or click an object to give the selected person its default action.
  3. Long-press or right-click for more actions, including talking to another person.
  4. Drag to move the camera. Pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.
  5. Turn on Queue, or hold Ctrl or Cmd, to add actions instead of replacing them.
  6. Use Clear orders to remove the selected person's waiting actions.
  7. With the game view focused, use arrow keys to choose a target, Space to select a person, and Enter for actions.
  8. Open the selected person's name to show or hide the needs panel.
