      How to play

      Game time is paused while this guide is open.

      1. Choose a person under Household to select them and see their needs.
      2. Tap or click an object to give the selected person its default action.
      3. Long-press or right-click for more actions, including talking to another person.
      4. Drag to move the camera. Pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.
      5. Build pauses the household. Move or rotate furniture, then Confirm or choose another item. Choosing another item commits a valid move and cancels an invalid one. Cancel leaves it unchanged; Exit build returns to play.
      6. In Build, Walls turns the line between two floor tiles into a wall, a doorway or nothing. Remove clears a wall; the status line says why when a change is not allowed.
      7. In Build, Room builds a whole room at once: choose two opposite corner tiles, then a line of the outline for its doorway, and press Build room.
      8. In Build, Buy lists everything for sale with its price. Choose something, point at the floor, turn it with Rotate, and press Buy; the price comes out of Funds. Items the household cannot afford are greyed out.
      9. A new action goes first; the actions already waiting continue after it. Turn on Queue, or hold Ctrl or Cmd, to add actions after the waiting ones instead.
      10. Use Clear orders to stop the action you gave the selected person and remove their waiting actions.
      11. With the game view focused, use arrow keys to choose a target, Space to select a person, and Enter for actions.
      12. Open the selected person's name to show or hide the needs panel, and Traits under it to show their traits.
      13. Open People to see how the selected person feels about everyone else.
      14. Open Options, the gear at the top right, for Light, Build, Sound, Save, Load, Clear orders, Queue, New game and Help. Light: flat gives neutral daylight without the automatic lighting cycle; Sound mutes everything; Effects sets cue and footstep volume.
