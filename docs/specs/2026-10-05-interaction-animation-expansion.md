toilet use, ordinary sitting, watching television and listening to the radio.
Preserve the accepted character, four facings, recolourable materials, saved
furniture identities and playable footprints. Deliver independently usable
batches after source, physical-contact, graphics and played review.

## Aquarium motion

Keep the accepted cabinet, glass, plants, stones and three fish. Author an
eight-sample closed swimming loop with different phases for the three fish.
Move each fish's complete body, eyes and attached tail together; articulate
the tail without separating its root from the body. Keep every fish contained
and visible below the lid in all four views. Render actual model rotations
at the existing camera scale. Preserve every published sprite and append the
new frames after the complete existing atlas. Use simulation ticks for phase;
Pause freezes motion and reduced motion holds the quiet first sample.

## Media seats

front. A usable seat must be unclaimed, reachable, face the device and have
an unobstructed view. Rank eligible seats deterministically by distance and
stable identity. Keep the active interaction owned by the television or radio;
reserve its seat separately. Never replace the media target with the chair.

seat before using the standing fallback. Cancellation, completion, selling,
moving and interrupted work must release seat ownership. Save and Load must
restore the same valid seat or apply a documented deterministic recovery.
Dining and ordinary chair users must see media reservations as occupied.

estimated height. Toilet use needs supported hips and a plausible leg chain.
Bathing needs a supported recline inside the tub. Showering needs feet inside
the tray and restrained washing motions. Use opaque bath water or steam for
coverage; do not add explicit anatomy. Preserve the accepted face and hair.

Render reciprocal visible body, fixture and outline contributions wherever
partial occlusion requires them. Keep body colour independent from furniture
colour. Check complete evaluated bodies against named furniture solids, then
compare actual rendered composition with an independent complete-scene render.
Keep drawing, picking, selection markers and activity bubbles registered to
the same displayed body. Check entry, loop, Pause, reduced motion, Load and exit.
