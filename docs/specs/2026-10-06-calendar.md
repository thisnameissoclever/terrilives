# Calendar: weekdays, weekends and working days

Status: implemented on branch `twcl/calendar`; the evidence is in `2026-10-06-calendar-verification.md`. This is the contract for the first slice of [S-calendar] in `docs/GAME-SYSTEMS.md`, written on 2026-10-06 against main `920abaf4` during autonomous roadmap work. Dates, months, seasons, birthdays, bill days and scheduled visits are later slices; this one gives the game a seven-day week and lets a job have working days.

## [CAL-week] The week is derived from the clock

A day is `day_ticks` ticks (1440) and day index `d` is `tick / day_ticks`, which the HUD already shows as "Day d + 1". The weekday is `(d + first_weekday) % 7`, where `first_weekday` is a tuning number from 0 (Monday) to 6 (Sunday) naming the weekday of day 1; the shipped value is 0, so a new game starts on a Monday and days 6 and 7 are the first weekend. Nothing is saved: the tick is already saved and hashed, and the weekday is a pure function of it and content. The function lives in `terri-core` beside `SimClock` so the simulation and the boundary share one definition.

## [CAL-careers] A career names its working days

Each career in `content/careers.toml` gains `working_days`, a list of weekday names from `mon` to `sun`, at least one, none repeated. The office job works `mon` to `fri`. The compiler stores it as a seven-bit mask. A shift starts only when the day clock reaches `shift_start` on a working day; on any other day the worker stays home and nothing else about the day changes. A shift already running when a weekend begins finishes as normal, since every shift is shorter than a day. Career content is not part of the save fingerprint, like every other balance value, and changing working days changes no saved state's meaning.

## [CAL-hud] What the player reads

The household clock reads `Day {n}, {Weekday}` on one line and `{hh}:{mm}` on the next, with the full weekday name, for example `Day 1, Monday` above `06:00`. The clock always takes those two lines, so the household panel does not change height at midnight. In the narrow desktop panel the `Time` label sits on its own line above the clock, which gives the clock the panel's full width; the wider compact panel keeps the label beside it. The selected person's Career row in Sim details reads the job label followed by its schedule: `Office clerk, Monday to Friday, 06:00 to 14:00` when the working days are one contiguous run, otherwise the day names listed with commas. Functional text stays plain.

## [CAL-evidence] Proof required before delivery

1. Weekday arithmetic at every day boundary for `first_weekday` 0 and 6, including the first tick of a day and the last, and a day length other than 1440.
2. Compile: an empty or repeated working-day list and an unknown name are refused; the shipped job compiles to the Monday to Friday mask; `first_weekday` 7 is refused.
3. Shifts: on a 30-tick test day, a career working Monday to Friday pays five times over seven days and the weekend days pay nothing, with bounded tick loops and clock assertions; a career working only `sun` pays once; the shipped lot's worker leaves on day 1 and stays home on day 6; every existing career, street, edit, bed and reservation test still passes without change because days 1 and 2 remain working days.
4. Determinism: the bare-agent golden hash and the pinned released-save hash are unchanged; two worlds run under the same content agree; the trace example still runs.
5. Boundary and HUD: `weekday_index` for the current tick and the career schedule projection cross the wasm boundary; the clock text and the career row render in the fake DOM tests; a displayed check at desktop and phone widths shows the new clock text and career schedule, with screenshots.
