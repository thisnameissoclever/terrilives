# Build mode: building a whole room in one edit

Status: [RT-slice-room] shipped in PR 97 at merge `e2f60a9`.

This is [WT-slice-room-rectangle] in `docs/specs/2026-09-21-wall-tool.md` and
the "rooms" part of [B-builder] in `docs/FEATURES.md`. The Walls tool (PR 95)
changes one line between two tiles at a time. Building a room that way takes a
dozen presses, and the house is refused or half-built in between: a wall that
would cut a sim off is refused on its own even when the finished room would
have a doorway.

## What the player gets

* **A Room tool.** A fourth Build tool, beside Furniture, Walls and Buy. The
  player chooses two opposite corner tiles, and the room's tiles are shown,
  tinted for whether it can be built.
* **A doorway in the same edit.** A click on a line of the outline makes that
  line the room's doorway; another click on it takes the doorway away.
* **One press builds it.** Build room turns every line of the outline into a
  wall, the chosen line into a doorway, in one edit that is either wholly
  applied or wholly refused, with the reason on the status line.

## What already exists, and is reused

* **Edge walls and doorways** in the saved `EdgeWallsV1` list, and the grid's
  edge barriers ([WT-apply]).
* **The rules for one wall** ([WT-rules]): the door-to-landing line, furniture
  cut in half, a sim standing across the line, a sim using something across
  it, the shared usability proofs, and the V3 loader's grid checks.
* **The line highlight** the Walls tool already draws.

## Decisions

### [RT-command] One command for the whole room

`SimCommand::BuildRoom { x0, y0, x1, y1, doorway }` is appended after
`BuyObject`. `(x0, y0)` and `(x1, y1)` are opposite corner tiles, in either
order, both inside the room. `doorway` is `None` or one line
`(axis, x, y)` as `SetWallEdge` names lines. It drains as a lot edit, in
stream order.

The outline is every line on the rectangle's edge that is an interior line of
the lot. Lines on the lot's own edge are the outside wall and are left alone,
so a room in a corner of the lot has two sides.

### [RT-rules] What is refused, and the order of the checks

1. `InvalidInput`: a corner or doorway number the boundary cannot read.
2. `UnsupportedLayout`: as for a wall.
3. `OutOfBounds`: a corner outside the lot.
4. `InvalidInput`: a doorway that is not a line of the outline.
5. The single-wall checks of [WT-rules], 4 to 7, each run over every new wall
   of the outline before the next check starts: `BlockedLanding`, then
   `WallOverlap`, then `SimOverlap`, then `InUse`. So the reason a player reads
   is the first kind of thing wrong, whichever line it is on. One function,
   `check_new_walls`, holds these for a single wall and for a room.
6. The shared proofs, once, on the whole candidate: the finished room, not
   each wall on its own. A room around furniture passes if its doorway lets a
   sim reach the furniture.
7. The V3 loader's grid checks, once, on the whole candidate.

A line already a doorway stays a doorway, so a room drawn across an existing
doorway keeps it. A line already a wall stays a wall, unless it is the line
asked for as the doorway, which opens. The doorway is never held to the rules
for a wall. A room whose outline is already built exactly is accepted and
changes nothing, and a room whose only change is opening its doorway only
removes a barrier and is not held to checks 5 to 7, as a single doorway is
not.

### [RT-apply] What a committed room writes

New lines are appended to the saved list in one fixed order: the top side
left to right, the bottom side left to right, the left side top to bottom,
the right side top to bottom. A changed line is updated where it is. The grid,
the saved layout and the lot revision change in one drain, as for a wall.

### [RT-hash] and saves

A staged `BuildRoom` is saved as `SavedCommand::BuildRoom`, appended, and the
world hash reads every field of it. The built walls are already in the hash
through [WT-hash].

### [RT-boundary] What crosses to the shell

`room_edit_preview(corners, doorway)` returns `[refusal, changes]`: the
refusal code or zero, and whether building the room would change the house,
so the shell can say a room is already built without deciding it itself.
Here `corners` is `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` and `doorway` is `[axis, x, y]` or empty
for none: two short arrays, because seven separate numbers is past clippy's
limit for one call. `build_room` with the same arguments stages the command.
`last_room_result` reports the corners and the refusal, then the doorway's
three numbers when there was one.

### [RT-shell] The Room tool

A fourth button in Build mode's tool switch, Room, between Walls and Buy; the
switch in `web/src/ui/build-tools.ts` now takes any list of tools. Two clicks
choose the corners, rather than a drag, because a drag already pans the
camera. The room's tiles are tinted valid or invalid, and a chosen doorway adds
the tile outside it, so the doorway shows as a step out of the room. Once both
corners are chosen, a click near a line of the outline toggles the doorway
there, and a click anywhere else starts a new room.

From the keyboard, the first arrow puts a corner mid-lot and the arrows move
it; Enter fixes it and the arrows then move the opposite corner; D steps the
doorway along the outline and back to none; Enter builds; Escape clears.
Build room is disabled while the room is refused, and for a room that is
already built, which says "This room is already built." A refusal that a
doorway could mend (someone's way, furniture, or a portal's landing cut off)
adds "Choose a doorway." when there is none, or "Try the doorway on another
line." when there is. Since [RD-root] in
`docs/specs/2026-09-22-reach-from-the-door.md`, a front door cut off is not one
of them: a room meets that refusal only while the door's tile is not open
floor, with something on it or off the lot. A built room clears the choice.
As with the other tools, a room on its way survives leaving the tool until
the drain reports it.

## What this does not do

Rooms that are not rectangles, deleting a whole room in one edit, floors, and
hinged doors ([WT-slice-interior-doors]).
