# Build mode: building a whole room in one edit

Status: working design. Nothing here is built yet.

This is [WT-slice-room-rectangle] in `docs/specs/2026-09-21-wall-tool.md` and
the "rooms" part of [B-builder] in `docs/FEATURES.md`. The Walls tool (PR 95)
changes one line between two tiles at a time. Building a room that way takes a
dozen presses, and the house is refused or half-built in between: a wall that
would cut a sim off is refused on its own even when the finished room would
have a doorway.

## What the player gets

* **A Room mode in the Walls tool.** Beside choosing one line, the player can
  choose two opposite corner tiles. The outline of the rectangle between them
  is shown, tinted for whether it can be built.
* **A doorway in the same edit.** A click on a line of the outline makes that
  line the room's doorway; another click on it takes the doorway away.
* **One press builds it.** Build room turns every line of the outline into a
  wall, the chosen line into a doorway, in one edit that is either wholly
  applied or wholly refused, with the reason on the status line.

## What already exists, and is reused

* **Edge walls and doorways** in the saved `EdgeWallsV1` list, and the grid's
  edge barriers ([WT-apply]).
* **The rules for one wall** ([WT-rules]): the door-to-landing line, furniture
  cut in half, a sim standing across the line, a sim using something across
  it, the shared usability proofs, and the V3 loader's grid checks.
* **The line highlight** the Walls tool already draws.

## Decisions

### [RT-command] One command for the whole room

`SimCommand::BuildRoom { x0, y0, x1, y1, doorway }` is appended after
`BuyObject`. `(x0, y0)` and `(x1, y1)` are opposite corner tiles, in either
order, both inside the room. `doorway` is `None` or one line
`(axis, x, y)` as `SetWallEdge` names lines. It drains as a lot edit, in
stream order.

The outline is every line on the rectangle's edge that is an interior line of
the lot. Lines on the lot's own edge are the outside wall and are left alone,
so a room in a corner of the lot has two sides.

### [RT-rules] What is refused, and the order of the checks

1. `InvalidInput`: a corner or doorway outside its range at the boundary, or a
   doorway that is not a line of the outline.
2. `UnsupportedLayout`: as for a wall.
3. `OutOfBounds`: a corner outside the lot.
4. Every line of the outline that would become a wall is held to [WT-rules]
   checks 4 to 7 in turn, in outline order, and the first refusal is reported:
   `BlockedLanding`, `WallOverlap`, `SimOverlap`, `InUse`.
5. The shared proofs, once, on the whole candidate: the finished room, not
   each wall on its own. A room around furniture passes if its doorway lets a
   sim reach the furniture.
6. The V3 loader's grid checks, once, on the whole candidate.

A line already a wall stays a wall. A line already a doorway stays a doorway,
so a room drawn across an existing doorway keeps it. A room whose outline is
already built exactly is accepted and changes nothing.

### [RT-apply] What a committed room writes

New lines are appended to the saved list in one fixed order: the top side
left to right, the bottom side left to right, the left side top to bottom,
the right side top to bottom. A changed line is updated where it is. The grid,
the saved layout and the lot revision change in one drain, as for a wall.

### [RT-hash] and saves

A staged `BuildRoom` is saved as `SavedCommand::BuildRoom`, appended, and the
world hash reads every field of it. The built walls are already in the hash
through [WT-hash].

### [RT-boundary] What crosses to the shell

`room_edit_preview(x0, y0, x1, y1, door_axis, door_x, door_y)` returns the
refusal code or zero; a `door_axis` of 2 means no doorway. `build_room` with
the same arguments stages the command. `last_room_result` reports the last
room the drain handled.

### [RT-shell] Room mode in the Walls tool

A two-way switch in the Walls panel, Line and Room. In Room mode the first
click chooses a corner tile and the second the opposite one. The outline's
tiles are ringed in the valid or invalid tint. A click on a line of the chosen
outline toggles the doorway there; a click anywhere else starts a new room.
Arrow keys move the second corner, D toggles the doorway on the nearest
outline line to the last click, Enter builds, Escape clears. Build room is
disabled while the outline is refused.

## What this does not do

Rooms that are not rectangles, deleting a whole room in one edit, floors, and
hinged doors ([WT-slice-interior-doors]).
