# Fights

Fights use the ordinary conversation approach, waiting, voice and duration. A player can select a Sim, open another Sim's action menu and choose Fight. Both bubbles show the subject in red. Completion lowers each participant's opinion and adds Had a fight; fights provide no Social or other need rewards.

## Autonomous causes and mood

A Sim can start an argument about an actual directed grievance:

1. Entering a room during private bathroom use, or starting private use beside an occupant.
2. An overdue chore currently assigned to the recipient, with unfinished work from the same continuous duty and a recorded complaint against that recipient.
3. A hated activity actually performed nearby, including television, music and exercise.
4. A conversation that actually interrupts an unhelped low or critical need. Shared enjoyment of a chat topic retains its exemption.

A bad mood alone supplies no culprit. Grief, a work setback, disliked plants and general incompatible interests do not manufacture blame. An assigned duty supplies no complaint until an observer actually reacts. A dish creator can differ from its assigned cleaner; a complaint against the creator cannot authorize an autonomous argument against somebody else.

Each idle initiator selects their strongest eligible grievance deterministically, with stable IDs breaking ties. They spend at most one seeded random draw per game minute. The shipped base probability is 0.001. Severity scales against 0.25 and is clamped to 0.1 through 2. Mood multiplies the probability by 2 at -100, 1 at zero and 0.25 at +100, linearly between those points. Opinion multiplies it by 1.5 at strongest dislike, 1 at neutral and 0.5 at strongest liking. The resulting probability is clamped to zero through one. The historical affinity threshold remains readable in old content; current arguments require a real grievance instead of that threshold.

Actual incidents remain eligible for six game hours. A privacy violation or interruption can prompt one argument; a continuous nuisance does not rearm after each minute. An unfinished chore can prompt another argument after one game day. Each participant's eight-hour Had a fight cooldown also prevents autonomous arguments. Neither side may be in a Hunger, Energy or Bladder crisis, at work or commuting, and the recipient may not be asleep, by the simulation's sleep state (`systems::circadian::is_asleep`). These rules constrain arguments without preventing later pleasant contact.

A pending autonomous order retains its exact incident and order ID. Both cooldowns are checked again before that order starts; another completed fight during the wait withdraws the automatic order. Resolving or reassigning its duty withdraws that order, including during an autonomous argument, with no completion losses or chore response. A player Fight admitted ahead of a pending autonomous Fight withdraws that exact automatic order. An automatic order that has not started is also withdrawn when its recipient falls asleep, through the same withdrawal as a crisis; the grievance is not marked as fought, so it can prompt another argument after the recipient wakes while it remains eligible. An argument already running finishes. This sleep rule is the coordinator's decision of 2026-10-24, applying the owner's ruling of 2026-10-23 on sleeping chat partners (issue 338) to arguments the game starts, which are not player orders. The published fight rules that authenticated historical content selects keep their original behavior. Player orders keep their ordinary commitment protections, including a player Fight with a sleeping recipient, which waits until they wake.

## Topics and outcomes

The chosen incident supplies the subject: dishes, another chore, privacy, television, music, exercise, another nuisance or an unmet need. Both preferences are -1. A player Fight can use either participant's relevant history from the last seven game days. When no history applies, a player Fight draws an ordinary subject; autonomous arguments never use random fallback. The subject does not claim that a new privacy violation or need interruption happened.

Existing fight topic codes 24 through 26 retain their published meaning. Current saves also accept the new subjects when an exact saved fight context validates them. Historical readers retain their original topic rules.

Completion applies the authored relationship loss of 0.1 in both directions and the Had a fight moodlet for 480 game minutes, with a mood penalty of 12. Central relationship scaling halves interpersonal losses for family and partners. An interrupted argument supplies no completion outcome. Presentation, voice clips and conversation length retain their existing rules.

## Chore response and repair

Finishing a relevant chore argument creates one four-hour repair window while that same assigned duty remains unfinished. The recipient makes one saved draw from the chore random stream. Neutral response probability is 0.80. Cleanliness, responsibility and commitment are centered on 0.5; chore preference, opinion of the initiator and mood are centered on zero. Their respective weights are 0.20, 0.10, 0.05, 0.05, 0.05 and 0.05. The final probability is clamped to 0.05 through 0.98.

Accepted work takes priority over the ordinary daily chore decision. It remains ordinary autonomous work: critical needs, work, book returns and player orders take precedence. A failed route ends the attempt. Waiting for another activity does not reroll the decision. A Sim who declined can still earn repair by doing the matching work within the window.

Every completed physical cleaning task earns each other living housemate 0.006 plus 0.006 times the observer's cleanliness in positive opinion, including with the board off and on repeat work. Actual dish washing earns that appreciation once per completed batch. Board accounting does not pay it again. Canceled work and zero completed units earn none.

Completing the argued duty in time adds 0.08 plus 0.04 times the affected Sim's cleanliness toward its actual completer, from the initiator and proven annoyed witnesses. The completer also receives that positive opinion toward the initiator. Beneficiaries are deduplicated across overlapping windows. Family and partner scaling applies once. A helper's work cannot pay another Sim's repair bonus.

Dish repair uses contributions for this continuous duty, separate from older board results. Largest actual contribution wins, with lower SimId breaking ties. The recipient must also have washed positive units during the repair window. Old loads cannot contribute to fresh dirt. Remaining original dishes preserve the continuous duty through partial washing, even after dirt falls below the owner's annoyance threshold. Cancellation, reassignment and replacement work cannot inherit a completed or invalid duty's bonus.
