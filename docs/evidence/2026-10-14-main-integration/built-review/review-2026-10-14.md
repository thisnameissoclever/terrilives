2. `node docs/evidence/2026-10-14-main-integration/built-review/check-narrow.cjs`: exit 0 with a FINDING receipt, rather than a passing verdict. The fresh viewport was 390 by 844. New housemate opened with its preview hidden. After selecting Review, Move in remained disabled for the bounded 20-second observation and the status text was empty. The source confirms the condition independently of the timeout: `CreatorView.drawPreview` starts preparation but returns when the preview is hidden; only a successful visible draw sets `previewReady`. Both submission and the Review button depend on that flag.

The repair should separate readiness of the current character's required contact data from whether its preview was drawn. Preparation must still reject stale results, errors, and destroyed sessions. Rendering a hidden preview is unnecessary to establish command readiness.
