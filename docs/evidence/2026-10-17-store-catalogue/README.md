Checked in the page as well: thirty cards, all enabled with $1,000,000 and all thirty grayed at $0, where a press on a grayed card chose nothing; nine cards under Kitchen; Clear filters pressed Every room and Everything, showed all thirty cards again and went disabled; a Clear filters press made while focus was on the Fun button left focus there, and a press while the button had focus moved it to Every room; the purchase reported Armchair bought. Every one of the thirty pictures measured 144 pixels on its longer side on its 144 pixel canvas, for example the Chapterhouse Wingback 100 by 144 and the exercise bike 140 by 144. On the phone the Buy tool's scroll width equaled its client width, 343 pixels each, with nothing chosen, with a card chosen and at $0, so the dock does not scroll sideways; the room row scrolled inside itself, 817 pixels of buttons in a 337 pixel row; the cards started 234 pixels below the dock's top; each phone card measured 68 pixels tall. At the pane's size the stuck footer's bottom edge sat 1 pixel above the panel's bottom edge, inside its border, and the Confirm button sat 21 pixels above that edge both in the middle of the scroll and at its end.

The pass found ten defects, all fixed before the final frames: the scrolling grid's rows took the cards' minimum height; the phone dock squeezed the strip shut; the Buy tool's rows in the dock shared the dock's height, so the Colour list overlapped the cards; the full-height cards did not fit the dock's short view, so phone cards now lay the picture beside the lines; the grid scrolled inside the scrolling panel, so two scroll bars stood side by side, and now only the panel scrolls; the Colour list showed with nothing chosen, against rule 4; the strip's negative margin made the phone dock scroll 2 pixels sideways, so the focus ring now sits inside the card; cards in one row took different heights when a model name wrapped, so the cards of a row now stretch to the tallest; the side panel's footer stuck inside the panel's padding, so scrolled content showed beneath it in every Build tool, a defect older than the catalogue, and the footer now carries the panel's bottom padding itself; and two seat sprites, the Chapterhouse Wingback and the exercise bike, record content bounds as wide as their canvas, so their pictures were drawn small, and every picture is now cropped to its visible pixels. The filters' pressed state is drawn in the accent color, and on the phone each filter row is one sideways row so the cards sit close below the filters. The pinned footer still passes over whatever scrolls behind it, as a pinned footer does; the end of the panel scrolls clear of it, as frame `05` shows.

On the phone preset the browser draws its overlay scroll bars a few pixels over the dock's right edge; that is the emulated touch device's scroll bar style, not page layout.
