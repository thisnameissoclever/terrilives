Checked in the page as well: thirty cards, all enabled with $1,000,000 and all thirty grayed at $0, where a press on a grayed card chose nothing; nine cards under Kitchen; Clear filters pressed Every room and Everything, showed all thirty cards again, went disabled and passed its focus to Every room; the purchase reported Armchair bought. A grayed card's lines measured an opacity of 0.8 and its picture 0.45. On the phone the Buy tool's scroll width equaled its client width, 343 pixels each, with nothing chosen, with a card chosen and at $0, so the dock does not scroll sideways; the room row scrolled inside itself, 817 pixels of buttons in a 337 pixel row; the cards started 234 pixels below the dock's top, against about seven wrapped rows of filters before; each phone card measured 68 pixels tall and each desktop card in the first row 150 pixels. On desktop the stuck footer's bottom edge sat 1 pixel above the panel's bottom edge, inside its border.

The pass found nine layout defects, all fixed before the final frames: the scrolling grid's rows took the cards' minimum height; the phone dock squeezed the strip shut; the Buy tool's rows in the dock shared the dock's height, so the Colour list overlapped the cards; the full-height cards did not fit the dock's short view, so phone cards now lay the picture beside the lines; the grid scrolled inside the scrolling panel, so two scroll bars stood side by side, and now only the panel scrolls; the Colour list showed with nothing chosen, against rule 4; the strip's negative margin made the phone dock scroll 2 pixels sideways, so the focus ring now sits inside the card; cards in one row took different heights when a model name wrapped, so the cards of a row now stretch to the tallest; and the side panel's footer stuck inside the panel's padding, so scrolled content showed beneath it in every Build tool, a defect older than the catalogue, and the footer now reaches over that padding. The filters' pressed state is drawn in the accent color, and on the phone each filter row is one sideways row so the cards sit close below the filters. The pinned footer still passes over whatever scrolls behind it, as a pinned footer does; the end of the panel scrolls clear of it, as frame `05` shows.

On the phone preset the browser draws its overlay scroll bars a few pixels over the dock's right edge; that is the emulated touch device's scroll bar style, not page layout.
