# Long-sleeve elbow repair

The creator's old long-sleeve selection combined a closed upper sleeve with a separately closed, enlarged forearm. Their end caps met at the elbow. The replacement uses one connected, subdivided shoulder-to-wrist garment per arm, with a smooth transition between upper-arm and forearm bone weights. Authored visibility conditions select the garment for covered forearms; bare, rolled and short variants retain the original skin. Hands remain independent and visible.

The source shoulder and wrist profiles remain intact. Rolled sleeves retain the original upper garment and cuff. Short sleeves retain their existing geometry. The accepted `sim-01-rigged.blend` and production atlas are outside this repair.
