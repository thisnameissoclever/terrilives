Checked in the page as well: the served bundle was `index-CmBeQXSB.js`; each frame's status line is quoted above as the page reported it; the console reported only the known missing favicon on the first load. Commit `85ac8c67a` changed only how the shader's numbers are written (4 became 4.0, 1e-4 became 0.0001, and leading zeros were added), not their values. As a check on screen, the marker regions of frames 01 to 04 and 07 were compared with lossless captures of the same moments on the build of commit `3e3e3f8a6`, taken during this session and not kept in the repository, and matched within two color levels; the only larger differences, in frame 04's corner, were a housemate's speech bubble that moved between runs.

The strip a few pixels wide along the base of the back wall in frame 04, where the coral rim and fill stop short of the wall, is the wall's baseboard: every straight wall carries one, 0.14 units high and standing 0.018 tiles out from the face (`assets/models/architecture/geometry.py`), and wall pixels write their own depth, so the floor behind it is genuinely hidden. The housemate marker on main is cut off the same way.

## Independent visual review

A fresh reviewer inspected the previous recapture (commit `db8c5aaf7`, the same marker rendering as this one) without being told which build it came from or what to expect beyond the marker rules. It found one ellipse per object with the spec's footprint shape (fitted to each rim within 0.56 to 0.74 pixels), matching colors, a constant border on every ellipse including the housemate's, and no rim drawn over anything standing in front of it. It asked for lossless frames, a refused placement at the toilet, and a correction to an earlier claim that no marker center was visible; this recapture answers all three. It also questioned the gap at the back wall, explained above.

A second reviewer judged twelve shuffled frames blind (`blind-review/image-A.jpg` to `image-L.jpg`): six from commit `bd7caf915`, before the short-wall and border fixes, and six from `db8c5aaf7`. `blind-review/KEY.txt` names each frame's source, and the reviewer did not see it. The reviewer called four frames defective, and all four came from the defective build: a coral rim across the short wall beside the toilet, a blue rim across the short wall left of a door, a rim tip climbing a short wall beside the bed, and a housemate border thicker at its ends than at its bottom. It passed every frame from the corrected build, and the two old frames that show no defect.
