# Outdoor furniture played pass, 2026-10-17

A played check of the pool, the pool lounger and the patio umbrella (issue 250, `docs/specs/2026-10-17-outdoor-furniture.md`) on the production bundle `index-Dd0DGKyd.js`, built from branch `twcl/250-outdoor` at commit `960dfd1b9`. Its WebAssembly module was built just before that commit merged main's pull request 288, which changed only comments, tests and one function's visibility in the simulation crate. From this build each outdoor sprite has registered depth in the `outdoor-depth` bundle, so frames 07 to 10 show the seated pose drawn with it.

The bundle was served by `vite preview` on port 4173 and driven in a Playwright-controlled Chromium window, because the desktop app's browser pane was hidden and could not draw. The household's money came from a save of the shipped lot written into the page's save slot; the clock ran for about 20 seconds while the page loaded, and the game was paused at Day 1, 17:28 with funds of 1,000,120. The game's sound was muted. The art itself was reviewed separately in `docs/assets/review-evidence/outdoor/2026-10-17/art-review.md`.

Frames are lossless 929 by 861 captures. Frames 02 to 07 and 09 are taken after one step of Zoom out and a middle-button drag that brought the yard in front of the house into view. Frames 08 and 10 are four times enlargements of frames 07 and 09.

The page reported these states, which the frames do not show as text. The first spot tried for the pool was refused with "Keep the entire object inside the lot." because it reached past the lot edge; the spot in frame 02 read "Ready to buy.", as did the lounger and umbrella ghosts, and each purchase read like "Patio umbrella bought.". The Furniture tool reported "Facing: South-west" for the bought lounger and "Facing: North-west" after one clockwise turn. The lounger's menu description reads "A wooden sun lounger with a padded teal cushion and a raised back." The console reported only the missing favicon.

Bill had no order of mine queued before Sit: his queue read "Going to read: The Quiet Moon", an action he had chosen himself. Sit was ordered at 17:28, and the queue first showed "Returning: The Quiet Moon" while he put the book back, then "Sit: Pool lounger" from 18:17 as he walked out; his activity read Sitting at 19:58. The pause landed after that sitting ended, so Sit was ordered again at 21:12 and a script in the page paused the game within 50 milliseconds of his activity reading Sitting, at 22:18. For frame 09 Bill first walked off, because the turned lounger needed the tile he stood on ("A Sim is standing there."); Sit was then ordered again and he sat at 23:23.

Not checked: a housemate walking behind the pool or under the umbrella's canopy, which no command can stage, and sitting with the lounger in its south-east and north-east facings. The action menu ignores a click from the same pointer that opened it, a guard for phones, and Playwright's mouse always reports the same pointer, so Sit was chosen by activating its menu button directly.
