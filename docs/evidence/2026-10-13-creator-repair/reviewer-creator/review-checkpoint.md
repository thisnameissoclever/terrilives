# Creator actual-play checkpoint, October 13

The desktop creator supports the main editing journey, but the Reading preview has a severe chair/body obstruction, the narrow navigation is broken, and several approved copy/formatting requirements remain unmet. This is an independent played review checkpoint, not owner approval or final acceptance. Production source was not edited by this reviewer.

## Findings requiring repair

1. **P1: Narrow navigation overlaps and can disappear.** At 390 by 844, opening a fresh new draft shows the seven page labels overlapping. Opening Show preview or visiting a long page collapses the navigation to a horizontal scrollbar. From an expanded Review, attempting to click Personality fails because the navigation/header/editor intercepts the click. The navigation measures 13 pixels high while its buttons are 44 pixels high; the editor starts immediately below those 13 pixels. A player cannot reliably choose pages by touch or pointer. Evidence: `11-mobile-blocked-navigation.png`, `14-mobile-fresh-draft.png`, `15-mobile-preview.png`. Likely repair surface: `web/src/ui/character-creator/creator.css`, narrow `.creator-nav` sizing and button shrinking.

2. **P2: Numeric settings and Review omit visible units and ranges.** Personality shows Cleanliness 78 without a percent sign or visible bounds. Body shows Height 90 without a percent sign. Review shows Hunger drain 102, Sleep timing -1.5 and activity values such as 1.35 without their units. The approved mockup provided ranges and units beside controls, and the runtime contract requires formatted percentages and multipliers in Review. Evidence: `02-personality.png`, `03-needs.png`, `10-review-expanded.png`, `saved-loaded-review.txt`.

3. **P2: Technical explanations remain in player copy.** Needs & habits says “Each activity value averages its available routes. Changing it sets every route in that activity to the same value.” Every activity repeats “0 prevents autonomous selection through this weight.” Hobbies say “Successful activities with this tag contribute to life satisfaction.” A new player needs to know which behavior changes and how strongly, without learning storage routes or tags. Evidence: `03-needs.png`, `04-interests.png`; captured text in the browser review output. Source pointers: `view.ts:172` and field descriptions under `web/src/character/`.

4. **P2: Trait-count instruction incorrectly says a removal is required below the limit, and the warning is repeated on every unavailable trait.** The note says “1 of 4 trait slots used. Remove a selected trait to make room.” The same removal instruction appears at two and three traits, although selecting another trait succeeds. At four, the same warning is appended to every unselected trait description, making the already long list repetitive. The owner explicitly rejected these repeated warnings. Keep one limit explanation beside the count, shown only at the limit. Evidence: `trait-rolls.json`, `02-personality.png`; source `web/src/ui/character-creator/view.ts:164` and `field.ts:16`.

5. **P3: Duplicate headings remain.** Identity repeats Identity above its one Name field; Household repeats Household; Needs & habits repeats Sleep timing as the group and field. The repair plan calls for removing duplicate headings. These repetitions consume vertical room and make the structure look unfinished.

6. **P1: Reading preview visibly intersects the chair across the upper body.** The chair's front and arm surfaces obscure most of the upper torso and cut across the arm/shoulder area. The book and hands remain outside while much of the body appears embedded in the chair. This is visible in both independently captured `anim-Reading-a.png` and `anim-Reading-b.png`. Reproduce with the captured draft: Male, height 90, build 3, shoulders 63, hips 71, torso proportions 65; select Reading, front-left facing. The owner supplied the exact corroborating annotated image at `C:/Users/myema/AppData/Local/Temp/codex-clipboard-638672cc-bb48-4379-a028-5763f4a7221d.png`, preserved here as `owner-reading-preview-annotated.png`. The reviewer initially failed to elevate this while preserving the earlier minor contact-overlap acceptance boundary. That was an incorrect judgment: this visible upper-body obstruction must not be described as accepted minor contact overlap. The images prove the symptom; they do not isolate whether its cause is body placement, fixture placement or depth.

7. **P2: Preview animation controls are cramped against their label and the miniature.** In the owner's annotated image, Animation nearly touches the Reading select, and the game-size preview begins immediately below the control row. The layout needs space between the label and select and between the controls and miniature. Evidence: `owner-reading-preview-annotated.png`. The desktop layout otherwise has ample unused horizontal space outside this cramped control row.

## Judgment against the original mockup

The implementation preserves the useful three-column arrangement and leaves the preview free of the rejected behavioral summary. Its desktop labels and controls are readable at 1440 by 1000. It loses the mockup's immediate numeric meaning because units and ranges are missing. Repeated technical helper text makes the long pages harder to scan, while the trait count tells the wrong story below the cap. The narrow navigation is an interaction failure, not a matter of visual preference.
