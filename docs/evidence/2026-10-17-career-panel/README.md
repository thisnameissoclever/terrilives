## Independent visual review

A fresh-context reviewer read the README and all seven images, enlarged the panel regions three to eight times and sampled pixel colors rather than trusting the small frames. Confirmed from the frames: the muted red title with the i control in `01` and `05`; every line of `01` on one line without clipping; the two-line red warning, the graph with its y axis, the dashed 65 and 40 rules, the seven-point line and the Week 1 and Week 7 labels, and the fieldset with Assign disabled and Quit enabled in `02`; in `03` seven evenly spaced points, a green dollar mark above week 2, green upward triangles above weeks 3 and 4, a red downward triangle below week 6, weeks 5 to 7 under the 40 rule, and week 7 at about 35 as `01` reports; the three checkboxes in `04` and `07` in the states the README names; the three career moodlets, the red Career row and the miniature with the same marks in `05`; the unemployed line, Assign enabled with Cafe still chosen, Quit disabled and Job left. in `06`.

Observations, none a failure: `03` is a crop at the same scale rather than an enlargement; the 65 and 40 labels touch the y axis; in `05` the Overview row's title wraps so that 16:00 and the i control sit alone on a second line, and the miniature's axis labels are only barely legible at that size; after Quit the path list still reads Cafe, so one click would assign again.
