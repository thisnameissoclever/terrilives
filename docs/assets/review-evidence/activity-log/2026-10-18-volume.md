This record holds the event volume that Task 7 of the [activity log plan](../../../superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-activity-log.md) measures, so the staging ring's size in [AL-bridge] of the [specification](../../../specs/2026-10-18-activity-log.md) rests on numbers rather than on the earlier research's estimate. The specification keeps no counts; this record does.

## What was run

- **Revision.** Branch `twcl/275-activity-log` at `cf3a19b64` (Tasks 1 to 6 on main `2b15dcfea`), plus the read-only volume counter this record's commit adds to `crates/terri-sim/examples/trace.rs` and `crates/terri-sim/examples/trace/activity_log.rs`. The counter only drains the log, once a tick, as the shell drains it once a frame.
- **Command.** `cargo build --release -p terri-sim --example trace`, then the built example run as `trace 28800 <seed>`: 28,800 ticks, which is 20 game days, of the shipped household on the shipped lot.
- **Seeds.** 20260728 (the shipped `rng_seed`), 7 and 42.
- **Machine.** Native Windows 11, Intel64 Family 6 Model 183; the counts do not depend on the machine.

The table's last row for seeds 7 and 42 is day 21, which holds only the records stamped at tick 28,800 by the day rollover; the means below cover the 20 whole days.

## Bursts and drops

| Measure | Seed 20260728 | Seed 7 | Seed 42 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Most records in one tick | 36 | 30 | 33 |
| Most records in 5 consecutive ticks | 36 | 30 | 36 |
| Records dropped by the staging ring | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Five consecutive ticks are the most one browser frame runs (`MAX_TICKS_PER_FRAME` in `web/src/main.ts`). For seeds 20260728 and 7 the five-tick maximum equals the one-tick maximum, so their peak came from one tick. The trace does not record which events made up a peak.

## Comparison with the research

The research estimated about 52 events and about 14 mess annoyances per game day. The measured volume is close: 45 to 64 records a day over 20 days, or 71 a day with a living household for seed 20260728, and 9 to 15 mess annoyances a day. A record is not exactly a research event: kind 6 duties and kind 8 feelings are children that add clauses to another line, so the number of lines the panel shows is lower than the record count.

## Decision on the ring size

The plan raises the ring when the largest five-tick count exceeds a quarter of the 256-record ring, which is 64. The largest measured five-tick count is 36, so the ring stays at 256 records.
