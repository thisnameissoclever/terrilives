# Sim 01 integration review

These are offline composites of actual exported sprites and authored furniture,
not screenshots of a played browser build. Enlarged nearest-neighbour sheets
expose native pixels; the source renders retain smooth high-resolution edges.

1. `chair-contact.png` is the rejected first contact pass, before correcting
   the ordinary chair facing and adding real near-surface foregrounds.
2. `chair-foreground-contact.png` compares the chair composition before and
   after its physical near surfaces are applied. Reproduce through
   `assets/sprites/gen/test_chair_foregrounds.py`'s `write_contact_review`.
   The ordinary chair and SE/SW wingback compositions are usable candidates.
   NW/NE wingback views still show an isolated wing fragment across the upper
   shoulder. Moving the seated torso forward did not eliminate it. Those two
   combinations are not visually accepted; do not erase pixels by height or
   move the Sim merely to conceal a depth-order defect.
3. `sleep-lower-bunk-contact.png` shows all four SE samples with and without
   the shipped upper-bunk foreground. Run `review_sleep_contact.py`. The body
   fits the mattress, but the compact knees and propped head read as cramped
   sleep. This does not prove compatibility with the other bed orientations.
4. `food-hand-contact-before-depth.png` is rejected. In NE the food appears
   over the rear head because a correct grip point was drawn at the wrong depth.
5. `food-hand-contact.png` is regenerated by `review_food_contact.py` using the
   current exported grip and depth order. Inspect every sample with both meal
   sprites before accepting it; generation alone is not validation.
   The current correction was inspected: NE food is properly hidden behind
   the head/body; the other three facings retain visible held meals. Ray tests
   and occluder names are recorded in the model's `food-depth-proof.json`.

Model action sheets and animated WebP loops live in
`assets/models/sims/sim-01/review/`. Full-resolution individual PNGs are
reproducible local evidence; native exports, sheets and animation loops are
retained for review. The approved source and editable rig are kept separately.
