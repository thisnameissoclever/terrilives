## Mechanism

Television and radio viewing already lease a physical chair: the planner
finds a seat inside the device's front cone whose own front faces the device,
routes the Sim to the tile in front of the chair, and the projection draws the
Sim on the chair's seat with the device as its target. Desk work now uses the
same lease with three differences. A work seat must stand against the desk:
the tile in front of the seat has to be part of the desk's footprint, so a
chair facing the desk from across the room does not qualify. Because that
front tile is the desk itself, the planner enters a work seat from any free
orthogonal side that is not part of the desk. A desk without a qualifying
seat keeps the ordinary standing use at the desk's edge, for player orders,
autonomy and privacy substitution alike, instead of falling back to a
viewing position, and its standing use holds no seat lease to maintain. Any
fitted seat type that stands against the desk and faces it qualifies, not
only the desk chair.

`seating::seated_activity` names every interaction that may hold a seat
lease (the two media uses and `desk.attend_correspondence`) and also decides
which travel and lease endpoints are reserved against other planners, so two
people cannot plan the same side tile. The media-only classification stays
in place for shared company and standing viewing contacts.

Seated desk work is presentation only. Unlike seated viewing, it neither
pays nor advertises the chair's Comfort, so desk work is chosen exactly as
often as before. `need_interactions::benefits` drops the seat rate for the
desk's work action when scoring, and `need_interactions::seat_rate` pays
nothing for a lease whose station is a desk.

Main declares each seat's approach tiles in content and persists a physical
seat claim per person. A work seat is entered from a free side, which is not
its declared front approach, so the claim validator, the claim migration for
older saves and the lease-free grid contact check each recognise a work
contact: an orthogonal side of a seat placed against the desk, inside the
desk's front cone and facing it, outside the desk's footprint, with an open
edge to the seat. The claim validator also requires the worker's lease on
that chair; the grid contact check cannot, because older saves reach it
before seat leases are restored, so it accepts a desk user mid-use on such a
tile on geometry alone. The live game never puts a leaseless worker there. The projection publishes `SIT` on the chair's socket with
the `CORRESPONDENCE` activity and the chair as the click target, so the web
draws the desk chair's fitted scene and picking separates body and chair
exactly as it does for television seating. The content fingerprint is
untouched because no content changed.

## Limits

If a seat lease becomes invalid during use and no seat qualifies any more,
the work is cancelled the way an interrupted viewing is, rather than
continuing standing; the standing tile beside a chair need not touch the
desk, so continuing there would not survive the save validator's contact
check. Moving, rotating or selling the desk or the chair is refused while
the lease holds; the lease can still fail during use when a wall is built
between the chair and the desk, or when the ordinary lease rules end it
(the worker leaves for work or a conversation, another Sim targets the
chair, or the chair becomes reserved).

Source review and local runtime proof do not establish public deployment.
