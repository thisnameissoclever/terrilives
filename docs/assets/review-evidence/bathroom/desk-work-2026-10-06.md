# Seated desk work, 2026-10-06

Base revision: `7fb45a816031208c33f611434f6054cbed8a8c67` (main after the hand-washing
change). This record covers seating the desk worker on a desk chair. It ships
no new art: the desk chair already has a fitted seated profile in all four
facings from the neutral seating batch.

## Decision

The missing-animation audit found desk Work drawing the standing idle beside
a desk that has a chair. On 2026-10-07 UTC the owner chose to seat the
worker on the desk chair with the existing seated profile, the way television
and radio viewers take a chair, and to author the long sofa's lying pose as a
separate batch.

## Mechanism

Television and radio viewing already lease a physical chair: the planner
finds a seat inside the device's front cone whose own front faces the device,
routes the Sim to the tile in front of the chair, and the projection draws the
Sim on the chair's seat with the device as its target. Desk work now uses the
same lease with two differences. A work chair faces the desk, so the tile in
front of it is the desk itself; the planner therefore enters a work chair
from any free orthogonal side that is not part of the desk. A desk without a
qualifying chair keeps the ordinary standing use at the desk's edge instead
of falling back to a viewing position, and its standing use holds no seat
lease to maintain.

`seating::seated_activity` names every interaction that may hold a seat
lease (the two media uses and `desk.attend_correspondence`); the media-only
classification stays in place for shared company, admissions and standing
viewing contacts. The projection publishes `SIT` on the chair's socket with
the `CORRESPONDENCE` activity and the chair as the click target, so the web
draws the desk chair's fitted scene and picking separates body and chair
exactly as it does for television seating. Save and load keep the lease
through the existing dining-and-seating validators; the content fingerprint
is untouched because no content changed.
