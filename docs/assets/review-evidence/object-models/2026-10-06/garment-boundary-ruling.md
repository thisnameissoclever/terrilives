# Garment source boundary ruling

The fresh read-only reviewer independently checked the corrective scene and
skin audit. The original sleeve has 1,730 evaluated vertices and 3,456 triangles;
the cuff has 338 and 672. Both are closed surfaces with Euler characteristic 2
and no boundary edges. They cannot establish a hollow sleeve through which
the unchanged forearm can pass. Historical modeling intent is unknown.

The 52mm radial correction also introduces real new forearm intersections:
53 and 52 newly contacted triangles, beyond the original closed-cap crossings.
The skin is unchanged. Neither accepting the baseline nor ignoring those new
contacts satisfies the geometry contract.
