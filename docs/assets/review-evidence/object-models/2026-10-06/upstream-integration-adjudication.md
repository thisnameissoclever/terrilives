and its confirmed P2 ordinary-device save-validation finding. The finding is
retained as a known limitation, not called fixed or merely unverified.

845101d and the current release accept the source-admissible two-viewer TV state

the current guard without an explicit continuation policy would make a migrated

covered. It does not claim complete uniqueness validation for every ordinary
device in imported or manually constructed saves. Keep purchase facts tied to
actual ordinary admission. The communal-activity roadmap now records the need
to reconcile those contracts together.

and its V6 continuation. It is constructed evidence accepted by published code,
not an authentic played capture, and must remain labelled accordingly. Do not
turn the regression into a promise that every forged current save must stay
accepted forever.

or implementation publication is authorized by this ruling. Complete the new
integration's targeted mutation and actual-browser gates, then return to the
catalogue task. Final whole-release review and owner content/visual approval
