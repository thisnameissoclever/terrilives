# Household chores, covered beds and seated evenings

Housemates can settle beneath bunk-bed blankets, watch television or listen
to the radio from nearby seats, and sit properly on the ottoman. Aquarium fish
swim with gentler, more continuous motion. Household chores cover dishes,
floors, surfaces and bins.

## Improved

- Housemates watching television or listening to the radio choose an available, reachable seat within seven tiles in front of the device. They stand facing it when no suitable seat is available.
- A seat used for television or radio remains occupied until its user finishes, leaves or cancels the action.

## Art

- Sims mop floors, wipe counters and tables with a cloth, and lift a tied bag from an opening bin. Cleaning pauses with the game and resumes from the same pose after loading.
- Cleaning poses keep readable eyes and fine facial outlines. Sims grip mop handles with closed hands above gathered cotton strands.

- Bunk-bed sleepers have relaxed legs and covered feet, with consistent views from all four directions.
- Aquarium fish drift and flex their tails independently in every view. Pause freezes the motion, and reduced motion holds a quiet pose.
- Seated viewers and listeners have fitted idle poses on dining chairs, desk chairs, reading chairs, armchairs, sofas and ottomans, with matching views and shirt colours.
- Sit down on an ottoman now shows a seated pose rather than a standing housemate.

## Fixed

- Selected sleepers are highlighted at their visible position rather than above their bed.

## New

- Click a dish pile to wash it, or right-click it and choose Do dishes. Other piles on the table stay put.
- Right-click a dirty counter or table and choose Clean up. The Sim returns for dishes added during the chore until that surface is clear.
- Queue dish chores with Queue mode or Ctrl/Cmd. Saves retain their targets and waiting orders.
- Footsteps and daily activities can dirty floors, counters and tables. Tidier Sims leave grime less often; unused areas stay clean. Choose Clean floor or Wipe surface to clean them.
- Food preparation fills bins. Choose Empty bin to clear one; waste waits if the household has no bin.
- Open Chores from the main bottom bar to see weekly assignments and daily outcomes, or send the selected Sim to do a chore now.
- Sims have separate responsibility, commitment history and preferences for each chore. Preferences affect enjoyment and weekly assignments; needs, mood and history affect daily follow-through.
- Housemates react to completed and neglected duties. The board records who actually did the work, including a housemate covering someone else's duty.

- Click the visible table or dishes beside a Sim without invisible corners of the Sim's sprite intercepting the click.
- Tables offer Sit when an adjacent chair is available, and Eat prepared food when the selected Sim has a portion to collect.
- Weekly wiping duties cover a room's counters or tables and update when furniture or rooms change. Automatic weekly assignments start enabled.
- Grime starts as faint stains, scuffs and smudges, growing more visible and bothersome as dirt builds up.

- Floor cleaning clears up to nine nearby tiles together, without reaching through walls. Grime fades as Sims clean, and interrupted work keeps its progress.
