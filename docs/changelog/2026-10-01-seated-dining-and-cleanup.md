# Seated meals and more reliable cleanup

Sims use real dining chairs and clean place settings, and take more responsibility
for dishes left around the house.

## Features & changes

- **Cleanup is more likely.** Average Sims clean their own dishes much more often. They can also notice old dishes they left themselves, and busy Sims retain a cleanup opportunity until they are free. Urgent needs still win over chores.
- **Meals use available chairs.** Diners sit at reachable chairs with clean settings. Full or dirty seats make them stand nearby; households without a reachable table can eat at a counter.
- **Dirty settings cause extra annoyance.** Someone forced to stand because of dishes can resent their creator and may clean the blocking dishes after eating. Average cleanliness gives this response a 30% chance before unmet needs reduce it.
- **Dining decisions survive saving.** Seats, dirty settings, deferred cleanup and interruptions retain their ownership and attribution.

## Art & sound

- **Food belongs in the action.** Cooking uses a stove pot and stirring utensil. Food transport and seated eating have separate animations, replacing the floating cooking plate.
