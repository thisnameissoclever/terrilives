# Windows, walls and floor materials

Build now offers nine window models with matching walls and distinct boards, tiles and carpet.

## Features & changes
- Choose Windows from the Walls tool, or press N from any Build tool. Models span one, two or three wall units on either axis. Use Fit window for a new opening, Replace window for the whole selected window, or Remove window to restore solid wall across its complete span.
- The chooser retains your selected model. Fit and removal report the applied result, including why an edit was refused.
- Historical window saves load as one-unit Sash windows. Saved floor choices keep their existing covering IDs.

## Art & sound
- Windows have authored frames, glazing and openings. Walls include thickness, joins, caps, reveals and baseboards. Cutaway windows follow their wall height while rear exterior windows remain full height.
- Interior Boards, Tiles and Carpet use separate material artwork with patterns aligned across neighboring tiles. Unpainted house floors use pale tile, yard uses grass and street uses asphalt. The new floor artwork also appears in historical saves without changing saved covering choices.

## Bug fixes
- Wall and Room edits cannot leave fragments of a wide window. Back returns from Windows with the clicked line selected for Wall, Doorway or Remove wall; W and D also work from the chooser. Remove wall opens the whole span where permitted. Doorway uses the clicked line and restores the rest to wall.
- Rear-shell windows can be selected from inside the lot. Window daylight contributes nothing at night, and Flat lighting remains neutral.
