# Windows, walls and floor materials

Build now offers nine window models with matching walls and distinct boards, tiles and carpet.

## Features & changes
- Choose Windows from the Walls tool, or press N from any Build tool. Models span one, two or three wall units on either axis. Use Fit window for a new opening, Replace window for the whole selected window, or Remove window to clear its complete span.
- The chooser retains your selected model. Fit and removal report the applied result, including why an edit was refused.
- Historical window saves load as one-unit Sash windows. Saved floor choices keep their existing covering IDs.

## Art & sound
- Windows have authored frames, glazing and openings. Walls include thickness, joins, caps, reveals and baseboards. Cutaway windows follow their wall height while rear exterior windows remain full height.
- Interior Boards, Tiles and Carpet use separate material artwork with patterns aligned across neighboring tiles. Yard, street and frozen legacy layouts retain their previous art.

## Bug fixes
- Wall and Room edits cannot leave fragments of a wide window. Selecting any covered line targets the complete window.
- Rear-shell windows can be selected from inside the lot. Window daylight contributes nothing at night, and Flat lighting remains neutral.
