# More personality around the house

Housemates have more varied habits, longer memories and a home full of new furniture.

## New
- Housemates make more varied choices. Self-preservation instinct affects how readily they respond to critical needs; choose a value or leave it random in New housemate.
- Open Personality and habits in Sim details to see sleep rhythms and how quickly each need falls and recovers.
- Sustained mood affects life satisfaction. Waiting for an occupied object lowers mood, especially when the need is urgent.
- Grief fades gradually over 10 to 60 game days, depending on the relationship.
- Death starts enabled in new games and is enabled once in older saves. Turning it off and saving keeps your choice.

## Improved
- Build and Options sit at the upper left. The bottom panel holds the selected person's details, actions and relationships.

## Fixed
- Early risers and night owls keep their sleep rhythms after saving and loading.
- Conversation audio stops with the correct conversation, fades at normal endings and retries missing voice recordings.
- Build previews and queued actions behave more consistently.

## Art
- Living-room furniture, dining furniture, chairs, the bookcase and kitchen bin have new artwork.
- The room layout and cutaway walls give a clearer view of the house.

## Sound
- Voices has its own volume control within the overall Effects volume.
- Showers and doors have recorded sounds, and activity sounds continue while the activity is in progress.
