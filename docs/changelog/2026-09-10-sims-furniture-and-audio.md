# Sharper Sims, furniture animations and sound

The household gains a shared rigged character model, distinctive shirt colours, more action poses and the first audio system.

## Art & sound
- **Sims have new models and action poses.** Walking, talking, eating, reading, sitting, watching fish, sleeping and cycling use artwork made for those activities. Tim, Bill and Casey have distinct shirt colours. [PR #60](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/60) and [PR #62](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/62)
- **Furniture animations support four facings.** The exercise bike and other supported furniture retain their authored views when turned. [PR #62](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/62)
- **The game has sound controls and conversation voices.** The first audio system adds gesture-enabled sound, an overall mute control and an Effects level. [PR #63](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/63)

## Bug fixes
- **Wall corners and doorways are clearer.** Wall joins, doorway edges, exterior alignment and visible corner definition were corrected. [PR #61](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/61)
