# Build rooms, buy furniture and choose family ties

Build mode gains rooms, doors, purchases, sales, colour choices and floor painting. New housemates can arrive with a family tie.

## Features & changes
- **Draw a whole room.** Choose opposite corners and a doorway to build a room. Interior doorways can become hinged doors. [PR #97](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/97) and [PR #98](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/98)
- **Buy and sell furniture.** Browse by the needs an object supports, preview purchases and their prices, and sell unused furniture for the displayed amount. [PR #96](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/96), [PR #100](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/100) and [PR #101](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/101)
- **Choose furniture colours and floor finishes.** Supported furniture can be recoloured before or after purchase, and individual floor tiles can be painted. [PR #104](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/104), [PR #105](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/105) and [PR #128](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/128)
- **Family ties are explicit.** New housemate can identify a partner, parent, child or sibling in the household. People displays the relationship, and saves retain it. [PR #131](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/131)
- **Options collects game controls.** Light, sound controls, Save, Load, New game and Help move into a collapsible panel. New housemate uses a paged form, and Traits starts collapsed. [PR #111](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/111) and [PR #110](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/110)
- **Furniture can show its type, model name and description.** The washing machine, armchair and dining table introduce this clearer identity display with descriptions reachable by pointer, keyboard and touch. [PR #122](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/122)

## Bug fixes
- **Building preserves access from the front door.** Edits that would strand furniture or block the front-door route are refused. Walls remain visible in the Walls tool, and placement buttons sit over the object being placed. [PR #99](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/99), [PR #112](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/112) and [PR #113](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/113)
- **Phone build controls stay reachable.** The compact build dock, disclosure targets and markers were revised for small screens. Rotated lamps and televisions retain their light. [PR #102](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/102), [PR #103](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/103), [PR #119](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/119) and [PR #123](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/123)
