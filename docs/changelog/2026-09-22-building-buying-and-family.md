# Build a home and choose family ties

Build rooms, shop for furniture and decide how new housemates fit into the family.

## New
- Draw a room by choosing opposite corners and a doorway. Add hinged doors to interior doorways.
- Buy furniture by the needs it supports, preview its placement and sell unwanted items.
- Recolour supported furniture and paint individual floor tiles.
- Add a partner, parent, child or sibling through New housemate. People shows the family relationship.
- Find lighting, sound, saving and Help in Options. New housemate has a paged form, and Traits starts collapsed.
- Furniture descriptions are available by pointer, keyboard and touch.

## Fixed
- Building refuses changes that block the front-door route or make furniture unreachable.
- Build controls and placement markers stay reachable on phones.
- Rotating lamps and televisions keeps their light working.

## Art
- The house has a yard and street. Housemates travel outside for work, and daylight comes through windows.
