# Fights, careers, seasons, yard furniture, and housemates who follow through

Housemates who cannot stand each other can now have it out, and you can start a fight yourself. The household can buy land beside the house. Every job is now a level on a career path, with weekly reviews that can promote, reward, demote or dismiss. The year has four seasons, and the Buy tool shows every item with its picture. The store also sells a pool, a pool lounger and a patio umbrella for the yard. Critical needs get more sensible choices, and a chosen chat waits for the other person to finish what they are doing.

## New

- Right-click a housemate while another is selected and choose Fight. The two square up as they would for a chat, both speech bubbles go red, and when it ends each thinks less of the other and feels bad about it for a while.
- Housemates start fights on their own, but only with someone they truly dislike, and only with a reason: a chore the other housemate was down for, skipped, and left undone. The bubble shows what the fight is about, the dishes or another chore. A fight does not get the chore done.
- A fight fills nobody's Social need. After one, a housemate holds off starting another for eight hours.

- Build mode has a Land tool. Choose a strip behind the house, on its left or on its right, read its size and price and what would be left, then use Buy land and confirm. The strip is ten tiles deep along the whole of that side, land costs more the more of it you own, and the house and everything in it stay where they are. Cancel at the bottom of Build mode, or Discard changes on leaving it, gives the land back and the money with it until you Confirm.

- Jobs sit on career paths. Three paths ship, the office, the cafe and the night watch, each with four titled levels and its own hours, working days, pay and top pay. The Career row in Sim details shows the current title and schedule.
- Every shift is scored from how well the job suits the person's traits and how easily they face other people, their skill at the work, and their mood as they leave. At the start of each week, every housemate who worked that week is reviewed on its scores. A good run can bring a promotion, which always raises pay, or a raise within the job; a poor week can bring a demotion; three poor weeks in a row can end in dismissal. Promoted, Got a raise, Demoted and Fired show in Mood for a while, and Warned at work shows from the second poor week.
- A job that suits a person badly wears on them: Hates the job appears in Mood and costs a little life satisfaction each shift. A job that suits them well brings Enjoys the job.
- Unemployed housemates look for work on weekday mornings, applying for the job that suits them best, and a housemate whose job suits them badly may move to a better path at a review. Both are on to start; Options has a box for each to turn them off.
- Sim details has a Career tab. It shows the path and title, the level, the pay and the level's range, the schedule, this week's score so far, the last review, what the score reads (fit, trait fit, social capacity against the job's demand, skill and mood), the streaks and what the next promotion needs, any warning, and a graph of the reviewed weeks with a green arrow at a promotion, a red arrow at a demotion, a green money mark at a raise and a gray diamond where they changed path. Choose a path and Assign to put the housemate on its first level, or Quit to leave the job; neither works while they are at work or on the way. The Career row in Overview turns the title green after three good reviews in a row and red after three poor, and its small i button shows the same graph.
- The year has four seasons, Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter, each seven days long, and a new game starts in Spring. The clock shows the season before the time, for example Spring, 06:00, and the yard's grass changes color with it: sun-dried in Summer, yellow-brown in Autumn and pale in Winter. A saved game opens in the season its day falls in. Seasons change nothing else yet.
- The top of Options shows the game's version number, starting with 1.0.0.
- The store sells three pieces of outdoor furniture, listed under the new Yard button in Room: a Pool, a Pool lounger and a Patio umbrella. Housemates can sit on the lounger; the pool and the umbrella are for looking at, so nobody can swim in the pool or shelter under the umbrella yet.

## Improved

- Housemates with several critical needs are much more likely to choose something that helps one of them. They keep their individual preferences and can still make the occasional terrible decision.
- A housemate who chooses to chat approaches and waits while the other person finishes their activity. Bathroom users get their privacy, work can delay the chat, and the activity display names the person being waited for.
- Random self-preservation values now favor the upper middle of the range. Values below 11 or above 95 require your choice in the creator or editor; existing housemates keep their saved values.
- The Buy tool in Build mode shows the store as a grid of cards, each with a picture of the item as it looks in the house, its type, its model and its price. Items the household cannot afford are grayed out. Room and Show are rows of buttons, and Clear filters shows everything again. On a phone the cards and each row of filter buttons scroll sideways. Choose item beside a placed ghost goes to the cards, and the arrow keys move between them.
- In Build mode, the item you are buying or moving now sits on one floor ellipse sized and shaped to fit it, like the one under a selected housemate. The ellipse is blue where the item can go and red where it cannot.
- Money is now kept to the cent. The screen still shows whole dollars: prices rounded up, funds rounded down.
- Confirm and Cancel at the bottom of Build mode's controls now return you to play once your changes are kept or thrown away. If Confirm cannot keep them, Build mode stays open and says why.
- Leaving Build mode another way while changes are not confirmed, with Exit build or Escape, now asks first: Confirm changes, Discard changes or Keep building. Exit build used to throw them away without asking.
- In Build mode, Escape and the Cancel beside a selection now clear only that selection and keep your other changes. Both used to throw away every change since the last Confirm. With nothing selected, Escape leaves Build mode.

- Existing saved games still load. The first save after this update keeps an untouched recovery copy of the previous save format.

## Fixed

- Choosing a chat with critical needs no longer traps a housemate in repeated canceled approaches, sliding back and forth while apparently deciding what to do.
- Finishing a chat removes its own player order, leaving later orders intact. Older saves may retain one extra chat where the original order cannot be identified safely.
- The final step of a walk keeps its walking pose until the movement finishes.
- Housemates with healthy needs no longer abandon a walk to make another random choice.
- Housemates who are cleaning no longer appear Idle.
- An unreachable chat no longer repeatedly blocks the other housemate's choices.
- The floor ellipse under a selected housemate now has the same thin outline all the way around, and the part of it that passes behind a cutaway wall stays behind the wall instead of showing on top of it.
- In Build mode's side panel, nothing shows through beneath the Cancel and Confirm bar anymore.
- A housemate who leaves for work partway through something, such as grabbing a snack, no longer shows that errand as their activity on the way to work, at work or on the way home. They pick it up again at home.
