# Fights over missed chores, and more land

Housemates who cannot stand each other can now have it out, and you can start a fight yourself. The household can also buy land beside the house.

## New

- Right-click a housemate while another is selected and choose Fight. The two square up as they would for a chat, both speech bubbles go red, and when it ends each thinks less of the other and feels bad about it for a while.
- Housemates start fights on their own, but only with someone they truly dislike, and only with a reason: a chore the other housemate was down for, skipped, and left undone. The bubble shows what the fight is about, the dishes or another chore. A fight does not get the chore done.
- A fight fills nobody's Social need. After one, a housemate holds off starting another for eight hours.

- Build mode has a Land tool. Choose a strip behind the house, on its left or on its right, read its size and price and what would be left, then use Buy land and confirm. The strip is ten tiles deep along the whole of that side, land costs more the more of it you own, and the house and everything in it stay where they are. Cancel or Exit build before Confirm gives the land back and the money with it.

## Improved

- Money is now kept to the cent. The screen still shows whole dollars: prices rounded up, funds rounded down.

- Existing saved games still load. The first save after this update keeps an untouched recovery copy of the previous save format.
