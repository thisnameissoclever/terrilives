# Fights over missed chores, more land, and careers with levels

Housemates who cannot stand each other can now have it out, and you can start a fight yourself. The household can buy land beside the house. Every job is now a level on a career path, with weekly reviews that can promote, reward, demote or dismiss.

## New

- Right-click a housemate while another is selected and choose Fight. The two square up as they would for a chat, both speech bubbles go red, and when it ends each thinks less of the other and feels bad about it for a while.
- Housemates start fights on their own, but only with someone they truly dislike, and only with a reason: a chore the other housemate was down for, skipped, and left undone. The bubble shows what the fight is about, the dishes or another chore. A fight does not get the chore done.
- A fight fills nobody's Social need. After one, a housemate holds off starting another for eight hours.

- Build mode has a Land tool. Choose a strip behind the house, on its left or on its right, read its size and price and what would be left, then use Buy land and confirm. The strip is ten tiles deep along the whole of that side, land costs more the more of it you own, and the house and everything in it stay where they are. Cancel or Exit build before Confirm gives the land back and the money with it.

- Jobs sit on career paths. Three paths ship, the office, the cafe and the night watch, each with four titled levels and its own hours, working days, pay and top pay. The Career row in Sim details shows the current title and schedule.
- Every shift is scored from how well the job suits the person's traits and how easily they face other people, their skill at the work, and their mood as they leave. At the start of each week, every working housemate is reviewed on the week's scores. A good run can bring a promotion, which always raises pay, or a raise within the job; a poor week can bring a demotion; three poor weeks in a row can end in dismissal. Promoted, Got a raise, Demoted and Fired show in Mood for a while, and Warned at work shows from the second poor week.
- A job that suits a person badly wears on them: Hates the job appears in Mood and costs a little life satisfaction each shift. A job that suits them well brings Enjoys the job.
- Unemployed housemates look for work on weekday mornings and take the job that suits them best, and a housemate whose job suits them badly may move to a better path at a review. There is no control yet to choose a job yourself or to turn these off; that panel is still to come.

## Improved

- Money is now kept to the cent. The screen still shows whole dollars: prices rounded up, funds rounded down.

- Existing saved games still load. The first save after this update keeps an untouched recovery copy of the previous save format.
