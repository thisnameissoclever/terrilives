# Day, night and sleep rhythms

The house has a day/night cycle, a new visual style and sleep behavior that follows each person's rhythm.

## Art & sound
- **The house changes with the time of day.** A new art pass adds a day/night cycle and distinct character looks. Sleep indicators are more legible. [PR #37](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/37) and [PR #38](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/38)

## Features & changes
- **Sleep follows a daily rhythm and reduces need drain.** Sleeping costs less than being awake, while the daily sleep drive helps guide bedtimes. [PR #37](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/37) and [PR #38](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/38)
