# Privacy and developing relationships

Housemates respect bathroom privacy more often, develop feelings through their interests and shared activities, and show shyness alongside their personality details.

## Features & changes
- **Bathroom boundaries have consequences.** Walking in during a shower, bath or toilet visit annoys its user. Starting beside someone already present annoys that person. The furniture's room defines the boundary.
- **Sims usually find another option or wait.** Urgent needs can override privacy avoidance; player orders keep priority. Shyness changes both avoidance and the reaction to an intrusion.
- **Interests shape relationships over time.** Pleasant nearby time helps, opposing preferences cause friction, and mutually enjoyable reading, exercise or aquarium watching helps more than proximity alone. Meals and TV do not yet receive this shared-activity bonus.
- **Inconvenient conversations can sour a relationship.** Interrupting someone with an unmet low or critical need reduces their opinion of the initiator.
- **Sleeping places retain ownership.** Assign a bed place from Personal details. Shared beds use separate approaches and leases; older supported saves keep their assignments and running actions. Occupied bed poses still need separate visual acceptance.

## Bug fixes
- **Sim details start narrower.** The sheet expands when Personal details need the space and returns to its compact width when those details close. Shyness appears with personality information.
- **Privacy detours preserve domestic tasks.** Meal pickup stays tied to the claimed counter, dining respects occupied seats, and interrupted cleanup resumes by collecting its dishes again.
