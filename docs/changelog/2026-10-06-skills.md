# Owned books, household chores and personal preferences

Buy books for the household and choose furniture by its model and useful details. Housemates can clean the house, develop skills and have their own likes and dislikes. Queued meals and chores wait their turn, the office job gets weekends off, and sleeping housemates have a recorded snore.

## New

- Everyone now has skills in cooking, fitness and reading that rise a little every time they finish the activity. Open Sim details to see each skill's level.
- Doing the same thing over and over now wears on a housemate's mood, and the longer they keep at it the worse it gets.
- Keep snacking past the point of hunger and a housemate feels sick for a few hours.
- The clock now shows the day of the week, and a new game starts on a Monday.
- Sim details now shows a job's working days and hours beside its name.
- Everyone now has likes and dislikes for plants, the aquarium, the television and the radio. Open Likes and dislikes in Sim details to see how each person feels about each one. Households in earlier saves get theirs when the save loads.
- Plants and the aquarium lift the mood of a housemate who likes them and sour the mood of one who dislikes them, while they share a room.
- A housemate who dislikes the television or the radio is bothered when someone else uses it in the same room, and likes that person a little less.

- Click a dish pile to wash it, or right-click it and choose Do dishes. Other piles on the table stay put.
- Right-click a dirty counter or table and choose Clean up. The Sim returns for dishes added during the chore until that surface is clear.
- Queue dish chores with Queue mode or Ctrl/Cmd. Saves retain their targets and waiting orders.
- Footsteps and daily activities can dirty floors, counters and tables. Tidier Sims leave grime less often; unused areas stay clean. Choose Clean floor or Wipe surface to clean them.
- Food preparation fills bins. Choose Empty bin to clear one; waste waits if the household has no bin.
- Open Chores from the main bottom bar to see weekly assignments and daily outcomes, or send the selected Sim to do a chore now.
- Sims have separate responsibility, commitment history and preferences for each chore. Preferences affect enjoyment and weekly assignments; needs, mood and history affect daily follow-through.
- Housemates react to completed and neglected duties. The board records who actually did the work, including a housemate covering someone else's duty.

- Click the visible table or dishes beside a Sim without invisible corners of the Sim's sprite intercepting the click.
- Tables offer Sit when an adjacent chair is available, and Eat prepared food when the selected Sim has a portion to collect.
- Weekly wiping duties cover a room's counters or tables and update when furniture or rooms change. Automatic weekly assignments start enabled.
- Grime starts as faint stains, scuffs and smudges, growing more visible and bothersome as dirt builds up.

- Floor cleaning clears up to nine nearby tiles together, without reaching through walls. Grime fades as Sims clean, and interrupted work keeps its progress.
- Buy individual books in Build mode. The store shows genre, length, price and the selected housemate's estimated interest. Unshelved books stay in household inventory until you put them in a bookcase.
- Housemates have individual reading tastes and remember their progress in each title. Switching copies keeps their bookmark and does not make a reread feel new.
- Reading uses an available owned copy. A housemate fetches it and returns it before starting another activity, including after an interruption.
- New households start with empty bookcases. Older saved households receive five starter books once at no charge; any that do not fit remain in household book inventory.
- Bookcases show the books actually shelved, with gaps while copies are borrowed.
- Click or tap a dropped book to open Books and recover that copy.

## Improved

- The office job now runs Monday to Friday. On Saturday and Sunday your clerk stays home.
- A housemate who can't cook, is out of shape or reads slowly now improves through their skill rather than the trait, so taking the trait away keeps what they learned.
- Furniture has a familiar type and a separate model name, with rewritten descriptions and buying details for price, capacity, benefits and requirements.
- Filter furniture by room in the store. Room associations do not restrict where you can place it.
- Furniture prices and benefits vary by model. The reading-specialist armchair improves comfort and entertainment while reading without changing reading speed or ordinary sitting.
- Immediate rereads provide less enjoyment. With no further reading, the benefit gradually recovers over two game years; chair comfort stays the same.
- Cooked meals finish sooner. A fridge, preparation counter and stove support cooking; a table and chairs provide optional seated dining.

## Fixed

- With Queue mode on, or when adding orders with Ctrl, repeated Grab a snack orders now run one after another. Each new order used to replace the snack already under way. The same holds for any meal or chore queued behind another: it waits its turn, and it counts among a housemate's waiting orders until it finishes.
- Object descriptions stay closed until you click or tap the name, or activate it with Enter or Space. Hovering or moving keyboard focus no longer opens them.
- Dining chairs share their occupied place with sitting and reading, so two activities cannot put different housemates on the same chair.
- Panning no longer consumes the next tap.

## Art

- Sofa reclining poses fit the cushions in all four directions.
- Sims mop floors with closed grips and gathered cotton, wipe counters and tables with a cloth, and lift a bag from an opening bin. Cleaning pauses with the game and resumes from the same pose after loading.

## Sound

- A sleeping household now snores: a real, deep snore every few seconds instead of the old breathing tone. It is low-pitched and plays at the same quiet level as the stove and the kitchen sink, so it can sit in the background if you leave the game running.
