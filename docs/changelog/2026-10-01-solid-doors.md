# Solid doors and cleaner crossings

Doors have a new model with visible thickness, panels and handles on both sides. Doorways on both wall directions now have hinged doors.

## Art & sound
- Doors swing through nine poses, with smoother transitions between simulation ticks.
- Door frames have continuous joins without stray lines across the posts.

## Bug fixes
- Door leaves and frames overlap Sims according to their visible surfaces during crossings.
- The floor threshold stays beneath feet, door leaves and frames.
- Horizontal doorways now show doors and use the same crossing and closing-sound behavior as vertical doorways.
