# Shared meals, room names and outdoor cleaning

Housemates appreciate the cook when they eat a shared meal. Name rooms across the whole lot, review boundary changes before keeping them, and clean paved areas outside.

## New

- Housemates appreciate the cook after eating a portion of a shared meal.

- Build > Rooms lets you select and name enclosed rooms or the shared Outside area. Room boundaries update when you Confirm.
- Splitting, merging or opening an existing room shows a review of the resulting names and cleaning duties. Back to build keeps your edits.
- Housemates can clean covered floors outside and wipe outdoor counters and tables. Grass and the street receive no floor cleaning.
- Floors offers Outdoor patio, a rectangular paving finish that can be cleaned indoors or outdoors.

## Improved

- Confirm and Cancel at the bottom of Build keep Build open. Confirm keeps the batch; Cancel restores your latest confirmed changes. Exit build handles returning to play.
- Affirmative actions use green controls; Back, Cancel and discard actions use red controls. Focus remains visible in compact room controls.
- Saving while you edit or review a build keeps the last confirmed arrangement. Older saved games retain their room labels and unfinished work, with recovery copies kept when their save format changes.

## Fixed

- A room name entered in review remains protected by the exit confirmation even after you reverse every wall edit.
- Trying to sell furniture that is in use keeps the refusal visible.
