# More rewarding company, grounded arguments and background play

Chats provide more Social and stronger relationship rewards. Family and partners are more forgiving, arguments come from real grievances, and fresh household mess gets a grace period. The game also keeps running while its tab is in the background. Build controls are clearer, and clipped Sim status can be opened in full.

## Improved

- Both Sims gain twice as much Social from chatting, up to 100. Conversation length stays the same, and fights provide no Social.
- Chats about a shared interest improve both Sims' opinions, including when other needs are low. Ordinary positive chat rewards are stronger too.
- Family and partners gain stronger positive feelings from social events and lose less goodwill from negative ones.
- Writing letters at a desk improves Social even when the writer is alone.
- All base need drain rates are reduced by one third.
- Fresh mess does not immediately lower mood or trigger complaints. Fully dirty sources get two game hours of grace; unfinished work gets a full game day. Sims can still clean during that time, and dishes blocking a seat remain an immediate problem.
- The grace period survives saving and loading. Saves from before this grace period was introduced receive fresh time for existing mess. New saves require this version or newer; recovery copies preserve the original older saves.

- Sims can argue about actual privacy violations, nearby disliked activities, interrupted needs and overdue assigned chores. Bad mood increases the chance; good mood and liking the other Sim reduce it. Both Sims get a break between autonomous arguments.
- After a chore argument, the assigned Sim may start cleaning. Completing that duty within four game hours helps repair feelings between the participants and with housemates who complained.
- Completed cleaning earns appreciation from other housemates, including with the chore board off. Cleaner housemates appreciate it more.
- Player-started arguments use relevant recent history, or an ordinary subject when none applies.
- Saving preserves argument causes, chore follow-through and partial dish contributions. New saves require this version or newer; the original older save remains available as a recovery copy.
- Confirm and Cancel at the bottom of Build now close Build. Confirm keeps your changes; Cancel discards them.
- Switching to Land asks you to confirm or discard other pending Build changes first.
- Hover over a clipped Sim status, focus it with the keyboard, or tap it to read the full text.
- Sim dock buttons take up less vertical space while keeping their widths and readable labels.
- Build controls omit repetitive selection and ready messages while keeping useful feedback.

- The game keeps running at the selected speed when you switch to another tab or minimize the browser, so the household carries on while you are away. A browser that suspends background tabs to save power can still pause it, and long pauses are not made up afterward.

## Fixed

- Loading an older saved game preserves chats that are waiting for a busy Sim, so the upgraded game can be saved and loaded again.

- Resolving or reassigning a chore stops an autonomous argument about its former owner. Older washing records cannot give a Sim credit for somebody else's fresh cleanup.

- Saved games from the previous version load correctly during a player-started argument.
- The Build zoom-out control displays a proper minus icon.
- Keep building uses a normal button in the exit dialog, and Confirm appears directly below Discard changes.

## Sound

- Game sound keeps playing while the tab is in the background. Choose **Sound: off** to silence it.
