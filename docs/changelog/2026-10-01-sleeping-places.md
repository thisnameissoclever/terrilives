# Sleeping places and covered double beds

Two Sims can share a double bed, and each Sim can have an assigned sleeping place.

## Features & changes
- **Assign a sleeping place.** Open Sim details, then Personality, habits and bed, to see each place's assignee and current occupant. Assign an available place or clear the selected Sim's assignment.
- **Sharing keeps separate places.** Walking to bed reserves one place. Cancelling one Sim's action leaves the other sleeper in bed. A tired Sim prefers their reachable assigned place, while player orders take priority.
- **Saves retain assignments and current sleepers.** Older supported saves load through the existing compatibility checks. Existing saves keep their furniture positions; new games place the double bed where both sides are accessible.

## Art & sound
- **Covered double-bed scenes.** All four facings show an empty bed, either sleeper, or both sleepers beneath one duvet. Each visible Sim can be selected separately. Furniture colour changes preserve their shirt colours. These are static sleeping poses.
