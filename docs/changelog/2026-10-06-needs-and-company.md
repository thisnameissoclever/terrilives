# Needs, rest and company

Needs now reflect real food, restful furniture, partial washing and enjoyable
company more consistently.

## Fixed

- Watching TV or listening to the radio fills Social only while sharing the same device with someone the receiving housemate likes.
- Eating fills Social only while seated with a liked housemate who is also eating in a chair facing the same table. Sitting at an empty table provides no Social.
- Chats fill Social only when the receiving housemate likes the other person. A first chat can still build friendship.
- Lonely housemates no longer treat solo television, radio or meal preparation as a reason to interrupt someone's private bathroom use. Waiting frustration follows the needs an activity can actually help.
- Seated television, radio and meals gain Comfort from the occupied chair. Eating while standing slightly drains Comfort.
- Washing hands improves Hygiene only up to 40. Bathing remains necessary for greater cleanliness. Hand washing no longer costs Comfort.
- Plain sitting and lying down provide no Fun. Reclining still restores Comfort and a little Energy.
- Bunk beds restore half the double bed's Comfort, alongside their Energy benefit.

## Improved

- Shared reading, exercise and aquarium watching slightly refill Social while liked housemates actively do the matching activity in the same room within four tiles. Both must accept the activity.
