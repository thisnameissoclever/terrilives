# Meals, shared dining and cleanup

Housemates prepare and cook meals, share food with hungry friends, and leave dishes that affect the people around them.

## Features & changes
- **Meals take time at several stations.** Sims get ingredients, prepare food, cook, simmer, plate the meal and eat at the dining table. Base station work takes 50 seconds before walking or waiting. The fridge offers breakfast, lunch or dinner according to the game clock.
- **Snacks need counter preparation.** Grabbing a snack now includes preparation and eating at a counter. Base station work takes 8.5 seconds before walking or waiting, with fewer dishes than a cooked meal.
- **A meal can feed four friends.** A cook can make extra portions for up to three hungry Sims they strongly like. The guests collect their food and gather at the same table; urgent needs and player orders still take priority. Dining uses the existing standing eating pose.
- **Cleanliness affects cleanup.** Cleanlier Sims are more likely to collect and wash their dishes. Very messy Sims rarely bother, and critical energy, hunger or bladder needs usually win even for a meticulous Sim.
- **Other people's dishes cause friction.** Dirty dishes lower visitors' mood and their affinity toward the person responsible. Cleanlier visitors are more annoyed and sometimes clean the mess themselves. Each new room visit permits one cleanup decision rather than repeated rolls every tick.
- **Meals and messes survive saving.** Food portions, dish ownership, cleanup claims and interrupted work are preserved. Existing meal saves continue at their corresponding updated stage.

## Art & sound
- **Cooking and cleanup have distinct motion.** Preparation, stirring, carrying plates and washing use separate animations. Used plates sit on the counter and dining table at furniture scale; collected dishes move into the cleaner's hands before washing at the kitchen sink.
