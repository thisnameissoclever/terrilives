# Create and customize your housemates

Build a new housemate or edit an existing one with animated previews and separate controls for appearance, personality, habits, and interests.

## New

- Customize gender, height, build, facial features, hair, clothing, colors, and details such as glasses and freckles. See the result in a large preview and at game size.
- Set temperament, traits, conditions, need rates, activity preferences, interests, skills, and household relationships in separate sections.
- New housemates start with randomized settings and a matching name from a pool of 100 names. Lock settings you want to keep, then randomize individual fields, sections, or the whole character.
- Undo restores the previous edit. Reset restores defaults, including ordinary 1.0x activity preferences.
- Preview Walking, Standing, Reading, and Cleaning animations. Walking plays automatically when the creator opens, with Pause and Play controls.
- Edit a living housemate's appearance and personality while preserving their progress. On narrow screens, you can finish creating or editing with the preview hidden.

## Improved

- Cleanliness affects tolerance for mess on floors, surfaces, and dishes, as well as willingness to clean.
- Activity preferences use familiar categories such as Read and Exercise instead of lists of individual books and furniture.
- Existing saved games remain loadable. Saving keeps an untouched recovery copy of the previous save format. New saves require this update or a newer version of the game.

## Art

- Customized characters use the same appearance in the creator and the house, with fitted clothing and continuous long sleeves.
- Furniture keeps its artwork and colors during interactions. Character and object edges stay smoother when zoomed, and activity bubbles follow the character's head.

## Fixed

- Dishes stay at the table setting where the meal was eaten.
