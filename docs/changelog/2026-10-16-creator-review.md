# Burnt meals, friendships over shared meals, and tidier creator numbers

Meals can now go wrong on the stove, doing things together matters more to how housemates feel about each other, and the character creator no longer shows long strings of decimals.

## New

- Meals can burn. When a housemate takes a meal off the stove there is a chance it is ruined: they throw it away, go back to the fridge to start again, and feel bad about it for a few hours. Practised cooks burn meals far less often, down to about one in five hundred at the top of the Cooking skill.

## Improved

- Eating at the same table and watching the same television or listening to the same radio now warm housemates toward each other at the same rate as reading, exercising or watching the aquarium side by side.
- Housemates who hate each other still have a chance to warm up over a shared meal or show.

## Fixed

- Surroundings preferences on the creator's Review page show at most two decimals, such as Likes (0.71).
- Activity preference values, and the trait influence note under each activity preference, show at most two decimals, such as 1.17× average.
- The value box beside each slider, and its tooltip, show the same tidy value as the Review page instead of a long decimal, and the tooltip now follows your edits.
