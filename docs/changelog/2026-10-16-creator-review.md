# Tidier numbers in the character creator

The creator no longer shows long strings of decimals.

## Fixed

- Surroundings preferences on the creator's Review page show at most two decimals, such as Likes (0.71).
- Activity preference values, and the trait influence note under each activity preference, show at most two decimals, such as 1.17× average.
- The value box beside each slider, and its tooltip, show the same tidy value as the Review page instead of a long decimal, and the tooltip now follows your edits.
