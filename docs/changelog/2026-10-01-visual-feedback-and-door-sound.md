# Activity bubbles, dock mood and quieter doors

Activity bubbles identify what housemates are doing, mood is visible in the Sim dock, and doors close with a softer impact.

## Features & changes
- **Active activities have distinct bubbles.** Icons above a visible Sim identify activities such as showering, reading, cooking, watching television and waiting. The bubble follows the displayed person's head as the camera moves. [PR #190](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/190)
- **Mood and life satisfaction stay visible in the dock.** The values remain visible when the dock is collapsed, while detailed moodlets remain in Overview. New housemate is available from Options, including when nobody is selected. [PR #189](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/189)

## Art & sound
- **Doors open silently and close with a softer thunk.** The high-pitched hinge sound is removed. [PR #188](https://github.com/thisnameissoclever/terrilives/pull/188)
