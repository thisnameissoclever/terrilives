# Requirements

This file is the requirements index. It points at the canonical acceptance
documents instead of duplicating their details and eventually disagreeing
with them, as documents tend to do when left unsupervised.

1. The playable alpha is defined by the eleven criteria in
   [alpha-goals.md](alpha-goals.md). Criteria 1 through 10 are implemented.
   Criterion 11 requires the owner's authored and approved voice pass.
2. Feature status and deferred scope live in [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md).
   Proposed M1 through M4 roadmap bullets are not automatically approved work.
3. Runtime, determinism, save, and ownership boundaries live in
   [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).
4. Human and automated acceptance gates live in
   [testing-protocol.md](testing-protocol.md).
5. Title, licensing, visual direction, AI-tool policy, backend identity,
   distribution, legal policy, moderation, and commissioned character art are
   owner decisions tracked in [TIM-TODO.md](TIM-TODO.md). Code completion does
   not silently decide them.
