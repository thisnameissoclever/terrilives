# UI elements

## Find the controls {#overview}

The user interface (UI) is the game's controls, menus and information panels. The world controls sit at the top left and the [Log](#log) button at the top right; the selected person and Household row sit in the bottom dock. Desktop can keep needs visible in that dock. On a phone, a selected person's needs are inside Sim details, then Overview. The separate down-chevron collapses the dock; the up-chevron expands it.

The paths below use the actual button and panel names. Desktop uses clicks and right-clicks; phone uses taps and long-presses. Scroll inside a panel when its content extends beyond the screen.

:::illustration overview

## Household and the selected person {#household}

Desktop: choose a name under Household in the bottom dock. Phone: use the Household name buttons along the bottom; expand the dock with the up-chevron if needed. Selecting a person changes the name, current activity, mood, satisfaction and all Sim details to that person's information.

Edit sits beside the selected name and opens that person's character draft. It is hidden without a selected person. Critical needs appear beside the current activity and remain visible when the dock is collapsed. A warning near a household name helps you spot another person who needs attention.

Click or tap empty floor to clear the selection. Hollow mood, life-satisfaction and need tracks mean no person is selected. On a phone, expand the dock to see the empty need tracks. Sim details and Queue require a selected person; Chores remains available for the household.

If the activity beside the selected name is clipped, hover over it, focus it with the keyboard, or click or tap it to read the full text. Escape or a press outside closes the popup. Reading it does not issue an order.

:::illustration household

## Clock, speed and camera {#clock-and-speed}

Read the top world panel for Funds, the Bills row and the three-line clock: day and weekday, time, then season and weather. The nearby speed controls pause or change the rate of play. These controls act on the whole household.

Drag the game view to pan. Use the mouse wheel on desktop or pinch on touch to zoom. In [Build mode](#build), the minus and plus buttons provide another way to zoom on both layouts. Weather follows game time, so Pause freezes it too. See [calendar and weather](the-world/#calendar).

### Bills {#bills}

The Bills row below Funds shows the amount to keep aside and the day the next bills are due, such as $425 due Day 8. While a bill is unpaid but within its grace period, it shows what is unpaid and the earliest unpaid bill's due day instead, such as $351 due Day 15. While a bill is overdue it shows the overdue total, late fees included, in red. Select it to open the Bills panel; the game pauses until you close it with Close or Escape. The row is unavailable in Build mode.

The Bills panel shows Funds, the amount to keep aside and whether Funds cover it, when unpaid bills still in their grace period become overdue, any overdue bills with their late fees, and the next bills with their amount so far and what each is expected to come to. Expand a bill to see its lines, such as the base charge, the tiles rented or each kind of use. Last month lists each bill and the day it was paid, or unpaid. How bills work repeats the rules. Payment is automatic, so the panel has no Pay button. See [bills](the-world/#bills).

### Player guide link {#player-guide}

Choose Player guide directly below Build and Options. The link remains available during Build. The guide opens in another tab. Options and Help also contain guide links. See [time controls](home-and-controls/#camera-and-speed).

:::illustration guide-shortcut

## Log {#log}

Choose Log at the top right to see what the household has done recently. Each line starts with the person it is about, such as Tim: Read a book or Bill: Annoyed by Tim's dishes. The oldest line is at the top and the newest at the bottom; the list follows new lines while you are scrolled to the bottom. The Log does not pause the game and stays open while you play. Close, Escape or the Log button closes it. Opening Options or Sim details closes the Log, and opening the Log closes them. On a phone, the open Log covers the screen down to the dock. The Log button is hidden in Build mode.

The filter buttons choose what the Log shows. Actions, Social and Career are on at first. Actions shows finished activities, chats and fights. Social shows chats, fights, activities shared with company, annoyances, deaths and how people felt about each other's chores and meals, so a missed chore that someone resents shows with the filters that start on. Chores adds the week's duties and the result of every chore, including chores done for someone else. Skills adds skill levels, and Career adds hiring, quitting and weekly reviews. Details adds numbers that compare with each other, such as opinion and Social changes. Time puts the day and time first, such as D2, T08:15. A line shows when any of its filters is on, and each filter adds its own part to the line. Your filter choices last until the page reloads.

:::illustration log

The Log shows the whole household. To follow one person, select them in the [Household](#household) row, then tick Only with their name, such as Only Tim, at the end of the filter row. The Log then shows only the lines that involve that person, either as the one who acted or as someone named in a shown part of the line. The checkbox follows the selected person and stays ticked until you untick it. With nobody selected, it is hidden and the Log shows everyone.

:::illustration log-only

The Log shows the newest 200 lines. Scroll to the top and choose Show earlier entries to add 200 more. It keeps a limited number of recent events, so the oldest events of a long session drop off. The Log is not saved: Load, New game and reloading the page empty it.

## Sim details and needs {#sim-details}

Desktop: select a person to see needs in the expanded dock, then choose Sim details for the fuller sheet. Phone: select a Household name, choose Sim details, then Overview to see all seven needs. Phone needs are not hidden inside Options.

Overview also shows mood reasons, Career, Doing and Orders waiting. The Career information button opens the [review window](#career-review-window), which pauses the game. Close returns to the dock. Changing the selected housemate changes whose information the sheet describes.

:::illustration sim-needs

## Mood, habits and bed {#schedule}

Open Sim details, Overview, then Personality, habits and bed. This disclosure gives personality and habit information, sleep timing and the assigned sleeping place. The mood reasons are higher in Overview.

The dock's mood meter describes the present. Its Life satisfaction meter describes longer-term wellbeing; hover or keyboard-focus it for the exact score. A No assigned bed condition means missing permanent ownership rather than merely an occupied mattress. See [Energy](needs/#energy) and [mood](mood-and-personality/#mood).

:::illustration sim-personality

## Queue and orders {#activity-and-orders}

Choose Queue in the dock, or Sim details, then Queue. Queue new orders appends new instructions when enabled. Clear orders cancels the selected person's current player-directed commitment and all waiting orders. The preview shows as many waiting actions as fit; it does not truncate the actual queue.

With no player orders, Clear orders is disabled. To change the current activity, give that person a new instruction.

On desktop, Ctrl or Cmd while issuing an action also appends it. With Queue new orders off, new instructions go first while earlier waiting ones remain. Doing in Overview identifies the current stage; an order such as Cook dinner can contain several stages. See [order priority](home-and-controls/#orders).

:::illustration sim-queue

## People {#people}

Open Sim details, then People. Each row is the selected person's feeling toward another housemate, with its named relationship band and direction on the meter. Select the other person to inspect their reciprocal feeling.

Warm does not prove both people like one another. Family is separate from the opinion. [Relationships](relationships/#directional-feelings) explains what changes each direction.

:::illustration sim-people

## Traits {#traits}

Open Sim details, then Traits. Each row names a trait and explains what it does. Capability rows show their matching skill; conditions show severity; disposition rows describe preference rather than a skill percentage.

Use Edit beside the selected name for changes. [Traits and conditions](mood-and-personality/#traits) explains why those rows behave differently.

:::illustration sim-conditions

## Skills {#skills}

Open Sim details, Overview, then Skills. This is a disclosure within Overview, not a separate top-level tab. Read Cooking, Fitness and Reading progress for the selected person.

Completed attempts add practice, including relevant failed attempts. Skills do not universally make every action faster or more rewarding. See [hobbies and skills](mood-and-personality/#hobbies-and-skills).

:::illustration sim-skills

## Likes and dislikes {#likes-and-dislikes}

Open Sim details, Overview, then Likes and dislikes. These responses concern objects and surroundings. They are separate from the People panel's feelings about individuals.

An object being present and another person actively using it can have different effects. See [mess and nuisance](relationships/#mess-and-nuisance).

:::illustration sim-likes

## Career {#career}

Open Sim details, then Career. Sim details is the same width on every tab on a computer screen, so switching tabs does not resize it. The panel shows the job title with its path and level, a Go to work box, a facts grid, Score inputs, and the Reviews graph with its key. Below those are the assign, shift and job-search controls.

The facts grid lists Shift, Pay, Pay range at this level, This week, Last review and Promotion. This week reads as the shifts worked and their score so far, or No shifts yet. Last review reads as a score out of 100, or None yet. Promotion counts the good weeks in a row toward the next level, or says the person is at the top of the path.

### Go to work {#go-to-work}

The Go to work box tells you when the selected person's next shift starts or started, and its button sends them to work now. The button is available from {{number:tuning.go_to_work_window_ticks}} game minutes before the shift starts until the shift ends. The person stops what they are doing and leaves, even with low needs, and a sleeper gets up. A housemate who is reading puts their book down where they stand instead of carrying it back first. Anything queued for them is cancelled.

Arriving early is on time. The time before the shift starts is not paid, and it counts as no missed work. Arriving after the shift has started counts the minutes between the start and arrival as missed work.

The button is unavailable, with a note saying why, when the shift is further away than that, when the person is already at work, on the way to work or on the way home, and when they have no shift in the next seven days. If the person has no way out of the house to work, the command is refused with a message. See [work and pay](the-world/#careers).

### Score inputs {#score-inputs}

Score inputs shows current Fit, Trait fit, Social capacity and Skill mastery, plus mood from the last scored shift. Fit runs from -1 to 1; Trait fit is one part of Fit. Social capacity shows the person's capacity beside what the job needs. Skill mastery is neutral at 50%. Mood on the last shift reads No shift yet until a shift has been scored. Rows with a value say Helps, Hurts or No effect, so you can see which inputs are pulling a score up or down.

Scroll to Assign a career, then choose a path and Assign to put the person at its first level, or Quit to leave their job. Shift lists By chance first and then each shift the path's first level offers, with its days, hours and chance of being drawn; leave By chance to let the hire draw one, or pick a shift to hold it. For an employed person with more than one shift to choose from, Shift schedule shows their shift; choose another and select Change shift. Their old shift is still worked that day if it has not started yet, and the new shift, like a newly assigned job, starts on a day after the day their last shift ended; after a night shift that ends in the morning, that can be two days after the change. Changing shift resets the good weeks counted toward the next promotion. These actions are refused while at work or commuting. That person's automatic job-search setting is here. The household's automatic career-change setting is in Options. Reviews continue when autonomy is turned off. See [work and pay](the-world/#careers).

:::illustration sim-career

:::illustration career-shift

Scroll to Reviews for the graph of actual events. Hover over a week, or tap it on a phone, to see its score and what kind of week it was. With the graph focused, Left, Right, Home and End move between weeks.

:::illustration career-review

### Review window {#career-review-window}

The information button beside the job title opens the review window. The same button is beside the Career row in Overview. Opening the window pauses the game, and the window says so. Closing it with Close, Escape or a click outside it resumes the game at the speed you had chosen before. If the game was already paused, it stays paused, and the window says that too.

The window shows each recorded week's review score, with contributing job titles when those details are available. Filters chooses which score inputs appear as their own lines. Skill mastery, Mood on shifts, Missed work and Fit are on at first, and Trait fit and Social capacity are off. On the score graph you can hide the Good and poor lines and the Promotions and other events. A Key explains the point colors and event markers. Pay per shift is listed separately, under Also show, because pay does not affect the score. Show chooses Last 4 weeks, Last 12 weeks or All weeks, and a summary beside it gives the average and the number of good and poor weeks.

Hover over a week, or move through the weeks with Left, Right, Home and End, to see that week's score, its kind and the inputs behind it. On a phone, tap a week to open its details, and use Filters to open the filter list. A week with no recorded inputs reads not recorded. The current job record keeps up to {{number:tuning.career_input_history_weeks}} weeks with recorded inputs. Assigning a career starts a fresh record; quitting or dismissal removes it. An automatic career change preserves it.

For a week spanning jobs or pay rates, the details list the jobs and the base-pay range. The pay line uses the salary held immediately before the review; night premiums and deductions are separate. Some older saves lack the original base-pay detail.

Fit, Trait fit, Social capacity, Skill mastery, Mood on shifts and the jobs' social demands are averaged across scored shifts. Missed work adds up the missed minutes.

## Chores {#chores}

Choose Chores in the bottom dock. The dialog pauses game time. Automatic weekly assignments enables or disables the board's assignment system. The weekly board shows each duty, owner, outcome and actual dirt. Do now sends the selected person to available work; select someone before using it.

:::illustration chores-board

Scroll within the dialog for the selected person's responsibility and chore preferences and Apply preferences. Open Recent daily outcomes below the assignments to inspect the history. Positive preferences mean enjoyment; negative ones mean dislike. These changes affect future daily decisions. Commitment history tracks follow-through separately.

The board's assignments do not make cleaning a personal Hygiene action. See [housework](activities/#housework) and [chore opinions](relationships/#chores).

:::illustration chores-profile

## Object and person menus {#object-menu}

Desktop: right-click a placed object or another person. Phone: long-press the target. With the game view focused, Enter opens the keyboard target's menu. A normal click or tap on an object issues its default action.

:::illustration object-menu

Actions surround the object. More actions opens the next page; Back returns. Close, Escape or an outside press dismisses the menu. Sim-dependent actions need a selected person.

On a crowded screen, the menu uses a row that scrolls sideways. Use its scrollbar, swipe the row, or press Tab to reveal each control. Close stays beside the row.

The object's type is primary and its model name secondary. Click or tap the object's name, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to read its description. Activate the name again to close it. The surrounding menu shows a separate description bubble; the compact row shows the description inside that row. Scroll within a longer description to read the rest.

:::illustration object-description

Enter build mode selects that exact object in Furniture, including objects without ordinary Sim actions. Bookcases offer automatic reading and book commerce. Clear orders appears when the selected person has a player-directed commitment or waiting orders to cancel. Autonomous activity can continue after cancellation. Close dismisses the menu.

Person menus offer Chat and Fight.

:::illustration person-menu

## Build mode and Furniture {#build}

Choose Build in the top world controls, or Enter build mode from an object menu. The household pauses. The Build dock replaces normal activity controls with a tool strip, status and Confirm/Cancel controls.

:::illustration build-furniture

Furniture selects a placed item. Choose a destination, use curved arrows to rotate, and Place. Colour changes its appearance. Sell shows its payout. A nearby Cancel clears the selection. The bottom Confirm keeps the batch and exits Build; bottom Cancel discards the batch and exits Build. Exit build leaves, asking about an unconfirmed draft when needed. See [Build rules](home-and-controls/#build).

:::illustration build-placement

Selecting a bookcase also shows its Books count and **Buy book** and **Sell book** controls; scroll the Furniture panel to reach them. Those controls change the books, while the furniture's **Sell** control sells the bookcase itself. Book choices are automatic; see [books and reading](activities/#reading).

## Buy {#buy}

Choose Build, then Buy. Use the catalogue's visible filters to find a type and model, choose it, set a destination and turn the preview, then Buy. Its price comes from Funds; unaffordable items are disabled.

:::illustration build-buy

The catalog can scroll while the placement controls remain available. Back to catalog returns from the purchase preview to the product list, keeping its filters and position. Scroll the Buy panel for product options. An item must still satisfy placement rules. A purchased decoration does not gain a use action just because it costs money. See [furniture](home-and-controls/#furniture).

## Walls and Windows {#walls}

Choose Build, then Walls, then select an edge between tiles, or a second edge in the same row to select a run. The actions beside the selection offer wall, doorway, removal and window choices. Low and Full height set how tall walls are drawn while you build. Windows is reached from a selected wall edge; it is not a separate tool in the main Build strip. Choose Windows, select a model, then choose Place Window or Replace window for an existing window.

:::illustration build-walls

The status explains refusals. A window must fit entirely within a solid wall. Windows block travel while admitting daylight. Doorways allow travel. Changes remain in the Build draft until Confirm. See [walls and floors](home-and-controls/#walls-and-floors).

:::illustration build-windows

## Floors {#floors}

Choose Build, then Floors, then select a tile, or two opposite corner tiles for a rectangle. Choose a covering beside it to apply that covering within the draft. Remove clears the covering and replaces an underlying indoor floor with grass; underlying grass and street stay unchanged. Selecting a tile alone does not apply a covering. Confirm at the bottom keeps the batch and exits Build.

Missing materials show a spinner beside the floor actions while the edit waits. If loading fails, choose **Retry** or another covering. Retry continues the waiting edit if the selected tiles and draft still match. Changing either, choosing another covering, leaving Floors or loading another household cancels the waiting edit. With no tile selected, material recovery appears in the game view and leaves the floor unchanged.

:::illustration floor-actions

## Rooms {#rooms}

Choose Build, then Rooms. Select rooms lets you click a floor or choose a room from the list, then change its name. Build room changes to rectangle construction using opposite corners and an optional doorway.

:::illustration build-rooms

Room names remain pending across tool changes. Enter in the name field does not confirm them. A boundary change may open a room review at Confirm. Back to build preserves the draft and current tool, including Walls when a switch to Land opened the review. See [rooms and land](home-and-controls/#rooms-and-land).

:::illustration room-review

## Land {#land}

Choose Build, then Land. With pending changes, Switch to Land? offers Confirm changes, Discard changes or Keep building. Confirm changes keeps the current batch and opens a fresh Land batch; Discard changes undoes it and opens a fresh Land batch. Keep building preserves the current tool and draft.

Choose Behind the house, Left side or Right side and inspect dimensions, price and remaining Funds. **Buy land** opens a separate land-purchase confirmation. Choose **Buy** there to add it to the draft; the bottom Build **Confirm** keeps it and exits Build. Read the status when an expansion is unavailable.
:::illustration build-land

## Options {#options}

Choose Options in the top world controls. The panel contains the game version, Close Options, Light, the death checkbox, automatic career changes, sound controls, New housemate, Save, Load, Export save, Import save, New game, Report bug, Help, Player guide and Changelog. Player guide opens these longer instructions in a new tab.

Report bug opens an email draft to Tim with the game version, recorded error and newest logs that fit. Review the draft and describe the problem before sending it. To copy or download the larger retained report, open [Startup report](https://natural-causes.life/startup-report.html) in the same browser. See [loading failures](start-here/#loading-failure).

Light: auto follows the clock; Light: flat removes the changing light cycle. Sound mutes all sound. Effects changes the overall effects mix; Voices adjusts conversations within that mix. Opening Options does not pause the household. Escape, Close Options or a press outside closes it.

Queue new orders and Clear orders are in the selected person's Queue panel, not in Options. Automatic job search belongs to each person's Career panel. See [Options and accessibility](home-and-controls/#options).

:::illustration options

## Admin panel {#admin-panel}

The admin panel lets you change the household directly: needs, feelings, funds, bills and more. It is marked Cheat, but the game does not record that you used it.

Desktop: press the ~ key (Shift with the key left of 1). Touch screen: hold the bottom corner tile of the lot, the one lowest on the screen, for half a second. A quick tap there still selects what is on it, and dragging still pans. The hold always opens the panel on that tile, so to open the menu of something standing on it, hold another tile it covers. The panel does not open in [Build mode](#build); leave Build first. While the panel is open, Build is unavailable.

The game pauses while the panel is open. The panel says so, and says which speed returns when you close it. Close, Escape or ~ closes it and restores the speed you had before. If the game was already paused, it stays paused.

The panel has three tabs. Housemate and Relationships act on the housemate named in the picker under the tabs; the picker starts on your selected person. Household acts on the whole household.

- **Housemate** sets needs, life satisfaction, likes and dislikes, skills and traits, and the job, shift, pay, job satisfaction and good or poor weeks in a row, and turns Job search on or off. It clears repetition and ends timed moodlets such as Promoted or Had a fight. Mood is shown but cannot be set, because it follows from everything else. Changing a need leaves the mood it has already built up as it is; the new level adds to or takes from Mood from needs from then on.
- **Relationships** sets how the chosen person feels about each other housemate, sets one or all of those feelings to 0, or has them start a fight. The fight begins when the game resumes.
- **Household** sets Funds, edits, pays, adds or deletes bills, removes late fees and marks bills paid, and skips ahead.

:::illustration admin-housemate

:::illustration admin-relationships

:::illustration admin-household

On a phone, the Housemate tab starts with Mood and moodlets, Repetition, Likes and dislikes, Skills, Traits and Career folded, each with a short summary beside its heading where there is one. Tap a heading to open or close it. On a larger screen they are always open.

Sliders apply when you let go. The number box beside each slider applies when you leave it or press Enter. A change the game refuses leaves everything as it was and says why on the line below the controls, such as Four traits at most. Remove one first.

Undo steps back one change at a time through everything you changed since you opened the panel. Its label names the change it will undo. Closing the panel clears Undo.

Skip ahead by a number of hours, then choose Skip. The game runs through those hours quickly while you watch, and housemates live them as usual. Stop or Escape ends it early. It also ends if a housemate dies. Skipping cannot be undone and it clears Undo.

Changes are part of the game, so Save keeps them. See [Save and Load](#saves).

## Save and Load confirmations {#saves}

Choose Options, then Save to write the local household slot. Options, then Load opens confirmation before replacing progress. Options, then New game opens a separate confirmation before deleting the local save and starting again.

After Save, wait for Game saved. The status reports the result; read it before assuming the write or load succeeded. The household slot belongs to this browser and site. See [saving](home-and-controls/#saves).

Choose **Load game** to restore the save. **Keep playing** dismisses the confirmation without replacing the household.

:::illustration load-confirm

Choose **Start over** to confirm a new game. **Keep playing** also dismisses this confirmation without replacing the household.

:::illustration new-game-confirm

Options, then Export save downloads the current household as a file and opens instructions for recovering a save from the old website. Follow the recovery link in the same browser, profile, and device where you played. Close the instructions with Close or Escape. Options, then Import save opens a file picker; after you choose a save file, a confirmation appears before the running game and this browser's saved game are replaced. Choose **Import game** to replace them. **Keep playing** dismisses the confirmation without changing anything. See [moving a save](home-and-controls/#export-import).

:::illustration import-confirm

## Help and the full guide {#help}

Choose Options, then Help. The short reference groups Build & place, People & actions, Company & chores, Camera, Options & saves, Full player guide and Shortcuts. Expand the relevant heading and scroll within the dialog. Choose **Got it** to return to the game.

Open the full player guide for activity effects, relationship causes and the screenshots on this page. The guide can be read without running the game. Changelog is a separate update-history destination.

:::illustration help

## Character creator {#creator}

### New housemate and Edit {#new-housemate}

Exit Build before adding or editing a housemate; New housemate and Edit are disabled while Build is active. Choose Options, then New housemate, or select a living person and choose Edit beside their name. The creator initially opens Appearance. Its section navigation offers Identity, Appearance, Personality, Needs & habits, Interests & skills, Household and Review.

On desktop the preview sits beside the controls. On a phone use Show preview to reveal it, then scroll within the creator for the controls below the portrait. Turn changes the preview direction; Animation selects a sample; Pause or Play controls that preview. These preview controls do not advance household life.

:::illustration creator-preview-controls

Randomize respects locks. Reset section restores the current page's defaults; Undo restores the previous operation. **Continue** moves through pages without applying the character early. Cancel discards the draft. See [adding and editing people](home-and-controls/#housemates).

### Identity {#creator-identity}

Identity edits the name and identity fields.

:::illustration creator-identity

### Appearance {#creator-appearance}

Appearance groups Colors, Body, Style, Face and Details. Open a group to reach its controls.

:::illustration creator-appearance

### Personality {#creator-personality}

Personality combines temperament and traits. Open Conditions to reach those settings.

:::illustration creator-personality

### Needs and habits {#creator-needs}

Needs & habits contains need rates and sleep habits. Open Presets, Advanced need rates or Activity preferences for their controls.

:::illustration creator-needs

### Interests and skills {#creator-interests}

Interests & skills covers preferences, hobbies and skills. Open Skills to adjust skill values.

:::illustration creator-interests

### Household {#creator-household}

Household sets family ties. These are separate from each person's feelings toward the other housemates.

:::illustration creator-household

### Review and apply {#creator-review}

Review shows the assembled draft before applying it. Expand a section to inspect its settings. Choose **Move in** to add a new housemate. When editing an existing person, choose **Apply changes** instead. Moving between pages does not confirm the draft; wait for the application to finish before returning to the household. Cancel closes the draft without applying it.

:::illustration creator-review

## Work-conflict confirmation {#work-confirmation}

Give a new order that conflicts with a person's work due or underway. A paused dialog names every affected person, remaining shift time, and pay/performance consequences. Proceed sends the instruction; Cancel sends nothing and preserves the queues and attendance. It can cover both participants in a conversation.

Older accepted orders can already delay work without another prompt. Your order retains priority even through critical needs. See [orders and attendance](home-and-controls/#orders).

:::illustration work-conflict
