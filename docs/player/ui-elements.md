# UI elements

## Find the controls {#overview}

The user interface (UI) is the game's controls, menus and information panels. The world controls sit at the top; the selected person and Household row sit in the bottom dock. Desktop can keep needs visible in that dock. A phone uses a compact dock, with the needs inside Sim details, then Overview. Expand or Collapse changes how much of the dock is shown.

The paths below use the actual button and panel names. Desktop uses clicks and right-clicks; phone uses taps and long-presses. Scroll inside a panel when its content extends beyond the screen.

## Household and the selected person {#household}

Desktop: choose a name under Household in the bottom dock. Phone: use the Household name buttons along the bottom; Expand reveals more of the dock if collapsed. Selecting a person changes the name, current activity, mood, satisfaction and all Sim details to that person's information.

Edit sits beside the selected name. It opens that person's character draft. It is unavailable without a selected living person. A warning near a household name helps you spot a person who needs attention without assuming the selected person is the only one in trouble.

If the activity beside the selected name is clipped, hover over it, focus it with the keyboard, or click or tap it to read the full text. Escape or a press outside closes the popup. Reading it does not issue an order.

## Clock, speed and camera {#clock-and-speed}

Desktop and phone: read the top world panel for Funds and the three-line clock: day and weekday, time, then season and weather. The nearby speed controls pause or change the rate of play. These controls act on the whole household.

Drag the game view to pan. Use the mouse wheel on desktop or pinch on touch to zoom. In [Build mode](#build), the minus and plus buttons provide another way to zoom on both layouts. Weather follows game time, so Pause freezes it too. See [calendar and weather](the-world/#calendar).

### Player guide link {#player-guide}

Desktop and phone: choose Player guide directly below Build and Options. The guide opens in another tab. The game keeps running in the background, so use Pause first if you want the household to wait. Options and Help also contain guide links. See [time controls](home-and-controls/#camera-and-speed).

## Sim details and needs {#sim-details}

Desktop: select a person to see needs in the expanded dock, then choose Sim details for the fuller sheet. Phone: select a Household name, choose Sim details, then Overview to see all seven needs. Phone needs are not hidden inside Options.

Overview also shows mood reasons, Career, Doing and Orders waiting. The Career information button opens the review graph. Close returns to the dock. Changing the selected housemate changes whose information the sheet describes.

## Mood, habits and bed {#schedule}

Desktop and phone: Sim details, Overview, then Personality, habits and bed. This disclosure gives personality and habit information, sleep timing and the assigned sleeping place. The mood reasons are higher in Overview.

The dock's mood meter describes the present. Its Life satisfaction meter describes longer-term wellbeing; hover or keyboard-focus it for the exact score. A No assigned bed condition means missing permanent ownership rather than merely an occupied mattress. See [Energy](needs/#energy) and [mood](mood-and-personality/#mood).

## Queue and orders {#activity-and-orders}

Desktop and phone: choose Queue in the dock, or Sim details, then Queue. Queue mode appends new instructions when enabled. Clear orders cancels the selected person's current player-directed commitment and all waiting orders. The preview shows as many waiting actions as fit; it does not truncate the actual queue.

On desktop, Ctrl or Cmd while issuing an action also appends it. With Queue mode off, new instructions go first while earlier waiting ones remain. Doing in Overview identifies the current stage; an order such as Cook dinner can contain several stages. See [order priority](home-and-controls/#orders).

## People {#people}

Desktop and phone: Sim details, then People. Each row is the selected person's feeling toward another housemate, with its named relationship band and direction on the meter. Select the other person to inspect their reciprocal feeling.

Warm does not prove both people like one another. Family is separate from the opinion. [Relationships](relationships/#directional-feelings) explains what changes each direction.

## Traits {#traits}

Desktop and phone: Sim details, then Traits. Each row names a trait and explains what it does. Capability rows show their matching skill; conditions show severity; disposition rows describe preference rather than a skill percentage.

Use Edit beside the selected name for changes. [Traits and conditions](mood-and-personality/#traits) explains why those rows behave differently.

## Skills {#skills}

Desktop and phone: Sim details, Overview, then Skills. This is a disclosure within Overview, not a separate top-level tab. Read Cooking, Fitness and Reading progress for the selected person.

Completed attempts add practice, including relevant failed attempts. Skills do not universally make every action faster or more rewarding. See [hobbies and skills](mood-and-personality/#hobbies-and-skills).

## Likes and dislikes {#likes-and-dislikes}

Desktop and phone: Sim details, Overview, then Likes and dislikes. These responses concern objects and surroundings. They are separate from the People panel's feelings about individuals.

An object being present and another person actively using it can have different effects. See [mess and nuisance](relationships/#mess-and-nuisance).

## Career {#career}

Desktop and phone: Sim details, then Career. The panel shows path, title, level, pay and range, schedule, this week's score, review history and the factors used by performance. The review graph marks actual events. The information button beside a Career title in Overview opens a smaller graph.

Choose a path and Assign to put the person at its first level, or Quit to leave their job. These actions are refused while at work or commuting. That person's automatic job-search setting is here. The household's automatic career-change setting is in Options. Reviews continue when autonomy is turned off. See [work and pay](the-world/#careers).

## Chores {#chores}

Desktop and phone: choose Chores in the bottom dock. The dialog pauses game time. Automatic weekly assignments enables or disables the board's assignment system. The weekly board shows each duty, owner, outcome and actual dirt. Do now sends the selected person to available work; select someone before using it.

Scroll within the dialog for the selected person's responsibility and chore preferences, Apply preferences and Recent daily outcomes. Positive preferences mean enjoyment; negative ones mean dislike. These changes affect future daily decisions. Commitment history tracks follow-through separately.

The board's assignments do not make cleaning a personal Hygiene action. See [housework](activities/#housework) and [chore opinions](relationships/#chores).

## Object and person menus {#object-menu}

Desktop: right-click a placed object or another person. Phone: long-press the target. With the game view focused, Enter opens the keyboard target's menu. A normal click or tap on an object issues its default action.

The object's type is primary and its model name secondary. Activate the name for its optional description. Actions surround the object. More actions opens the next page; Back returns. Close, Escape or an outside press dismisses the menu. Sim-dependent actions need a selected person.

Enter build mode selects that exact object in Furniture, including objects without ordinary Sim actions. Bookcases offer automatic reading and book commerce. Person menus offer Chat and Fight. The Nothing action cancels selected-person orders; it is not the menu's Close control.

## Build mode and Furniture {#build}

Desktop and phone: choose Build in the top world controls, or Enter build mode from an object menu. The household pauses. The Build dock replaces normal activity controls with a tool strip, status and Confirm/Cancel controls.

Furniture selects a placed item. Choose a destination, use curved arrows to rotate, and Place. Colour changes its appearance. Sell shows its payout. A nearby Cancel clears the selection. The bottom Confirm keeps the batch and exits Build; bottom Cancel discards the batch and exits Build. Exit build leaves, asking about an unconfirmed draft when needed. See [Build rules](home-and-controls/#build).

Selecting a bookcase also shows its Books count and **Buy book** and **Sell book** controls. Those controls change the books, while the furniture's **Sell** control sells the bookcase itself. Book choices are automatic; see [books and reading](activities/#reading).

## Buy {#buy}

Desktop and phone: Build, then Buy. Use the catalogue's visible filters to find a type and model, choose it, set a destination and turn the preview, then Buy. Its price comes from Funds; unaffordable items are disabled.

The catalogue can scroll while the placement controls remain available. An item must still satisfy placement rules. A purchased decoration does not gain a use action just because it costs money. See [furniture](home-and-controls/#furniture).

## Walls and Windows {#walls}

Desktop and phone: Build, then Walls, then select an edge between tiles. The actions beside that edge offer wall, doorway, removal and window choices. Windows is reached from a selected wall edge; it is not a separate tool in the main Build strip.

The status explains refusals. Windows block travel while admitting daylight. Doorways allow travel. Changes remain in the Build draft until Confirm. See [walls and floors](home-and-controls/#walls-and-floors).

## Floors {#floors}

Desktop and phone: Build, then Floors, then select a tile. Choose a covering beside it or Remove to restore the original floor within the draft. Selecting a tile alone does not apply a covering. Confirm at the bottom keeps the batch and exits Build.

## Rooms {#rooms}

Desktop and phone: Build, then Rooms. Select rooms lets you click a floor or choose a room from the list, then change its name. Build room changes to rectangle construction using opposite corners and an optional doorway.

Room names remain pending across tool changes. Enter in the name field does not confirm them. A boundary change may open a room review at Confirm. Back to build preserves the draft and current tool, including Walls when a switch to Land opened the review. See [rooms and land](home-and-controls/#rooms-and-land).

## Land {#land}

Desktop and phone: Build, then Land. With pending changes, Switch to Land? offers Confirm changes, Discard changes or Keep building. Confirm changes keeps the current batch and opens a fresh Land batch; Discard changes undoes it and opens a fresh Land batch. Keep building preserves the current tool and draft.

Choose Behind the house, Left side or Right side and inspect dimensions, price and remaining Funds. **Buy land** opens a separate land-purchase confirmation. Choose **Buy** there to add it to the draft; the bottom Build **Confirm** keeps it and exits Build. Read the status when an expansion is unavailable.

## Options {#options}

Desktop and phone: choose Options in the top world controls. The panel contains the game version, Close Options, Light, the death checkbox, automatic career changes, sound controls, New housemate, Save, Load, New game, Help, Player guide and Changelog. Player guide opens these longer instructions in a new tab. Pause first if you want the household to wait while you read.

Light: auto follows the clock; Light: flat removes the changing light cycle. Sound mutes all sound. Effects changes the overall effects mix; Voices adjusts conversations within that mix. Opening Options does not pause the household. Escape, Close Options or a press outside closes it.

Queue mode and Clear orders are in the selected person's Queue panel, not in Options. Automatic job search belongs to each person's Career panel. See [Options and accessibility](home-and-controls/#options).

## Save and Load confirmations {#saves}

Desktop and phone: Options, then Save to write the local household slot. Options, then Load opens confirmation before replacing progress. Options, then New game opens a separate confirmation before deleting the local save and starting again.

The status reports the result. Read it before assuming the write or load succeeded. The household slot belongs to this browser and site. See [saving](home-and-controls/#saves).

Choose **Load game** to restore the save, or **Start over** to confirm a new game. **Keep playing** dismisses either confirmation without replacing the household.

## Help and the full guide {#help}

Desktop and phone: Options, then Help. The short reference groups Build & place, People & actions, Company & chores, Camera, Options & saves, Full player guide and Shortcuts. Expand the relevant heading and scroll within the dialog. Choose **Got it** to return to the game.

Open the full player guide for activity effects, relationship causes and the screenshots on this page. The guide can be read without running the game. Changelog is a separate update-history destination.

## Character creator {#creator}

### New housemate and Edit {#new-housemate}

Desktop and phone: Options, then New housemate, or select a living person and choose Edit beside their name. The creator initially opens Appearance. Its section navigation offers Identity, Appearance, Personality, Needs & habits, Interests & skills, Household and Review.

On desktop the preview sits beside the controls. On a phone use Show preview to reveal it. Turn changes the preview direction; Animation selects a sample; Pause or Play controls that preview. These preview controls do not advance household life.

Identity edits the name and identity fields. Appearance groups Colors, Body, Style, Face and Details. Personality combines temperament and traits. Needs & habits contains need rates and sleep habits. Interests & skills covers preferences, hobbies and skills. Household sets family ties. Review shows the assembled draft before applying it.

Randomize respects locks. Reset section restores the current page's defaults; Undo restores the previous operation. **Continue** moves through pages without applying the character early. Cancel discards the draft. See [adding and editing people](home-and-controls/#housemates).

On Review, choose **Move in** to add a new housemate. When editing an existing person, choose **Apply changes** instead. Moving between pages does not confirm the draft; wait for the application to finish before returning to the household. Cancel closes the draft without applying it.

## Work-conflict confirmation {#work-confirmation}

Desktop and phone: give a new order that conflicts with a person's work due or underway. A paused dialog names every affected person, remaining shift time, and pay/performance consequences. Proceed sends the instruction; Cancel sends nothing and preserves the queues and attendance. It can cover both participants in a conversation.

Older accepted orders can already delay work without another prompt. Your order retains priority even through critical needs. See [orders and attendance](home-and-controls/#orders).
