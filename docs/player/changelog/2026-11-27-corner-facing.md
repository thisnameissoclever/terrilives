# Walking keeps its direction after a load

A walking housemate who is exactly on a corner keeps facing the way they just walked when you load.

## Fixed

- A walking housemate on a corner of their path no longer turns toward the next stretch for a moment after you load. They keep facing the way they just walked.
- Buying land while a housemate is walking does not turn them. They keep facing the way they were walking.
- A save from before this update still shows that brief turn when you load it. Saving again keeps the facing once that housemate has taken another step. Saving while they are still standing on the corner leaves the turn in place. Older saves still load.
