# Late book returns and Go to work

A housemate putting a book back no longer makes them more than an hour late for work, and books left on the floor get put away. The Career tab can send a housemate to work and opens reviews in a larger window.

## New

- Go to work in the Career tab sends a housemate to work now, whatever they are doing and however low their needs, once their shift has started or starts within two hours. Arriving before the start counts as on time. The time before the shift is not paid. A housemate who is reading puts the book down where they stand.
- The i beside a job title opens the review graph in its own window and pauses the game until you close it. Choose which score inputs to show and how many weeks. Pay is shown separately, because it does not affect the score.

## Fixed

- A housemate who was reading when their shift started could spend more than an hour carrying the book back across the house before leaving for work. They still put it back first, but if they are still carrying it an hour after the shift starts, they leave it on the floor where they stand and go to work.

## Improved

- A book left on the floor, by someone who had to leave for work or by someone who died, no longer waits for you to recover it. A housemate with nothing else to do picks it up and puts it back on its bookcase. Only one housemate goes for each book, and nobody does it while asleep, at work or busy.
- Hover over or tap a week on the Career tab's graph to see its score and what it meant.
- Score inputs, formerly What the score reads, says whether each input helps or hurts.
- Sim details keeps one wider size on every tab on a computer screen.
