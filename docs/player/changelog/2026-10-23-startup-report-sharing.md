# New address, save files, easier bug reports and work shifts

Natural Causes is now at natural-causes.life, saved households can move between browsers and devices as files, and error details can be sent from Options, the failure screen or Startup report page. Jobs now come with shifts, and there are five new careers.

## New

- Natural Causes has its own address: natural-causes.life. The old address opens the new one.
- Options has Export save, which downloads your household as a file.
- Options has Import save, which loads a save file after you confirm. It replaces the current game and your saved game in this browser.
- A save made at the old address can come along: open [Move your Natural Causes save](https://thisnameissoclever.github.io/natural-causes-old-save/) in the browser where you played, choose Download save, then use Import save at natural-causes.life. Do this soon, because some browsers clear saved data for sites you have not visited for a while.
- Five new careers: Hospital, Warehouse, Library, Restaurant kitchen and Buses, each with four levels.
- Jobs now come with shifts. Depending on the career, a job can be a day, early, late or night shift, and a new hire is given one by chance. Office work is nearly always a weekday day shift. Housemates leave for work and come home at their own shift's times.
- The Career tab in Sim details names each housemate's shift. When you assign a career you can pick a shift or leave it to chance. You can also change an employed housemate's shift while they are at home; the new shift starts the next day, and changing shift resets the good weeks counted toward their next promotion.
- Night workers sleep during the day. Early workers go to bed about an hour earlier, and late workers about two hours later.

## Improved

- Copy debug details copies the report for sharing with Tim. If copying is blocked, the details remain selectable.
- Email Tim opens a draft with the game version, recorded error and newest logs that fit.
- Report bug in Options prepares the same email while the game is open.
- Reports retain recent activity and errors after loading finishes.
- Housemates looking for work now try different careers by chance, still favoring the ones that suit their traits, rather than always picking the same best fit.
- Older saved games keep every job on its usual hours.

## Fixed

- Bug report emails fit Gmail's draft-opening link, with the recorded error first and newest logs ahead of older details.
