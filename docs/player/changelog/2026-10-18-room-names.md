# Shared meals, room names, outdoor cleaning and work shifts

Shared meals earn the cook some goodwill, and the game has a new logo. Name rooms across the whole lot, review boundary changes before keeping them, and clean paved areas outside.
Sims keep your orders through urgent needs, explain failures, confirm new orders that interrupt work, and no longer wait on each other until they starve. Each housemate now has their own setting for looking for work. Sims claim available beds, and condition numbers explain their mood effects. Jobs now come with shifts, and there are five new careers.

## New

- Winter has occasional brief snowfall, Autumn has falling leaves that fade from the ground, and Summer has occasional hot periods. The clock shows the season and current weather below the time.

- Housemates appreciate a living cook after eating a portion of a shared meal.
- Build > Rooms lets you select and name enclosed rooms or the shared Outside area. Room boundaries update when you Confirm.
- Splitting, merging or opening an existing room shows a review of the resulting names and cleaning duties. Back to build keeps your edits.
- Housemates can clean covered floors outside and wipe outdoor counters and tables. Grass and the street receive no floor cleaning.
- Floors offers Outdoor patio, a rectangular paving finish that can be cleaned indoors or outdoors.
- Five new careers: Hospital, Warehouse, Library, Restaurant kitchen and Buses, each with four levels.
- Jobs now come with shifts. Depending on the career, a job can be a day, early, late or night shift, and a new hire is given one by chance. Office work is nearly always a weekday day shift. Housemates leave for work and come home at their own shift's times.

## Improved

- The changelog has a larger logo and a compact header that brings the latest changes closer to the top.
- Sims without an assigned bed automatically claim a reachable, unassigned sleeping place. Existing bed assignments are preserved.
- Sim details explains each condition's mood points when you hover over or focus its number.

- Sims keep your orders when their needs become urgent. Busy housemates finish their current activity before taking incoming conversations, which appear in their queue. Needs continue to fall.
- Your orders take priority over work. New orders that delay or interrupt a shift ask for confirmation. Pay and energy use reflect time worked; missing more than two hours lowers performance, and missing the whole shift scores zero.
- A failed order gives a reason and briefly shows a frustrated squiggle above the Sim.
- Housemates looking for work now try different careers by chance, still favoring the ones that suit their traits, rather than always picking the same best fit.
- Job search is now set for each housemate: the Career tab in Sim details has a **Look for work automatically when unemployed** box, on for every new housemate. Options no longer has a job search box for the whole household; a saved game that had it off keeps job search off for everyone.
- Older saved games keep their unfinished orders when updated. New saves require this version or newer; recovery copies preserve the original older saves.
- Confirm and Cancel at the bottom of Build keep Build open. Confirm keeps the batch; Cancel restores your latest confirmed changes. Exit build handles returning to play.
- Affirmative actions use green controls; Back, Cancel and discard actions use red controls. Focus remains visible in compact room controls.
- Saving while you edit or review a build keeps the last confirmed arrangement. Older saved games retain their room labels and unfinished work, with recovery copies kept when their save format changes.

## Fixed

- Grass stays green in every season, with only a subtle seasonal tint.
- The missing-bed mood penalty applies only to Sims without an assigned bed.
- Windows can be installed while a Sim is fetching or reading a book.
- Saves preserve book fetching and reading when windows are installed.

- A room name entered in review remains protected by the exit confirmation even after you reverse every wall edit.
- Trying to sell furniture that is in use keeps the refusal visible.
- Housemates no longer stand waiting on each other until they starve. A Sim waiting to chat now steps aside for another Sim's fight or ordered conversation with them, then chats afterward.
- A fight a Sim starts over a missed chore is called off before it begins when either Sim becomes critically hungry, tired or desperate for the bathroom, or leaves for work. A fight already under way finishes, and fights you order still go ahead.
- A Sim who chose to chat with someone gives up waiting once they become critically hungry or tired, and will usually go and look after themselves. Sims also no longer start a chat of their own while critically hungry or tired. Chats you order still wait.
