# Object actions and automatic books

Object actions surround the selected object, and households manage their books automatically.

## Improved
- Right-click furniture, appliances or decoration to enter Build with that object selected, even when no Sim is selected.
- Object actions appear in a circle of buttons. Buy and Sell show their prices beneath the action.
- New households start with three distinct books scattered across their bookcase shelves.
- Buy book chooses a title and puts the copy on the bookcase with the fewest books. Sell book sells an available copy from the selected bookcase. Title and destination selectors have been removed.
- Household inventory fills available bookcases automatically. Borrowed books keep their reserved slots; copies without space stay in inventory.
- Sims prefer unfinished books, then unread books, then the book they read longest ago. They can fetch the preferred copy from another bookcase.
