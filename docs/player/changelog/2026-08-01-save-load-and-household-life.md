# Save progress and direct a household

Save your household, follow their working lives and plan what they do next.

## New
- Save and load one household in your browser, with an automatic save each game day.
- Select up to six housemates and open People to inspect their relationships.
- Traits, hobbies and work affect life satisfaction. Working housemates follow an office schedule and earn wages for the household.
- Activities such as cooking involve several steps and can resume after an interruption.
- Move and zoom the camera with a pointer or touch. Long-press for actions, queue orders, or choose targets with the keyboard.
- First-time Help explains the controls.

## Fixed
- A failed load keeps your current game. Saving and loading cannot overwrite one another mid-operation.
- Needs drain correctly at work, and the reading chair can be used.
