# Clearer chat bubbles and housemates who remember

Housemates now remember what each person likes to talk about, and chat bubbles agree with the Log and stay readable at night and on small screens.

## New

- Housemates learn what each person likes and dislikes talking about, and steer their chats toward the topics the other person enjoys and away from ones they dislike.
- A more sociable housemate remembers more of their chats and makes better guesses about someone they have not talked with yet.
- Memories fade over about six game months unless another chat about the same topic brings them back. Games saved before this update start with nothing remembered.

## Fixed

- A chat bubble now shows the reaction the Log names. A housemate who does not mind the topic shows a flat bar on a plain bubble, where before a small dislike showed a thumbs-down and a small like a thumbs-up.
- Chat bubbles and activity symbols keep their colors at night, so you can still tell a liked topic from a disliked one in the dark.
- On a crowded screen, overlapping bubbles move apart and a bubble a panel would cover moves just clear of it. Each stays within its own width of its housemate, so in a tight spot a bubble can still overlap another a little or stay partly behind a panel.
- The character creator's Needs & habits page no longer shows a warning about the pools.
