# Slower needs and full bathroom visits

Every need now runs down more slowly, and a toilet, shower or bath runs until the meter is full.

## Improved

- Hunger, Energy, Hygiene, Bladder, Social, Fun and Comfort all fall about 25 percent more slowly. A full meter lasts about a third longer.
- A visit to the toilet, shower or bath now lasts until the meter it fills is full: Bladder for the toilet, Hygiene for the shower and bath. A housemate who gets very little from it can reach a time limit first and leave with the meter not full.
- If a visit stops early, for example because you give another order, the housemate keeps only the gain from the minutes they spent.
- A visit is shorter when the meter is not empty when it starts. A shower from very dirty takes about 40 minutes of game time.
- A shower costs Energy and a bath gives Comfort for every minute you stay, so a longer visit costs or gives more.
- Washing hands is unchanged.
