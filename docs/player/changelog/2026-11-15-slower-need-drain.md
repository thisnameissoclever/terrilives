# Slower needs and full bathroom visits

Every need now runs down more slowly, and a toilet, shower or bath used until it finishes always fills its need.

## Improved

- Hunger, Energy, Hygiene, Bladder, Social, Fun and Comfort all fall about 25 percent more slowly. A full meter lasts about a third longer.
- A housemate who finishes using the toilet now always has Bladder at 100, and one who finishes a shower or bath always has Hygiene at 100, however low the meter started. Stopping early, for example because you give them another order, still leaves the meter partly filled.
- How long a toilet, shower or bath takes now depends on how far the meter has to rise. A nearly full housemate is quick, and a shower from very dirty takes under an hour of game time. Most visits are shorter than before, because housemates usually go before the meter is empty.
- The Energy a shower costs and the Comfort a bath gives arrive every minute, so a longer visit costs or gives more.
