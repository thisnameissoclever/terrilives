# Confirm or cancel build changes

Review your changes before keeping them.

## Fixed

- Build mode now keeps changes pending until you choose Confirm. Cancel or Exit build discards unconfirmed changes, including purchases and refunds.
- Cancel and Confirm sit together at the bottom of the build controls. Window placement now says Place Window.
- Rooms built against the edge of your land keep their outer walls. You can remove and rebuild those new walls.
- The Walls panel shows window controls only when you choose Windows, including on phones.
- Holding a finger on the game no longer selects menu text or opens the browser menu over the game actions.
- Releasing a long press no longer accidentally chooses a menu action that appeared beneath your finger.
