# Conversation subjects, swimming and bath toys

Housemates can swim, play with a toy in the bath, and chat about trains, gardening and work.

## New

- A chat can now be about trains, bicycling, gardening, the weather or, for a housemate with a job, work. Each housemate likes some subjects more than others, and brings up the ones they like.
- How a housemate feels about each subject comes from who they are: someone who enjoys exercise leans toward bicycling, someone who likes plants toward gardening, and someone happy in their job toward work. You cannot change these feelings.
- Sim details, Overview, Likes and dislikes now lists each subject after the object rows, from Loves to Hates. Work appears only for a housemate with a job.
- A subject chat shows the Talk symbol in the usual like or dislike color, and the Log names the subject, as in a chat about the weather.
- A listener reacts to a subject by their own feelings, so one housemate can love talking about trains while the other dislikes hearing about them. A listener who dislikes the subject thinks a little less of the speaker, as with any disliked topic.
- Choose **Hop in** from a pool menu, or let a housemate swim on their own. Swimmers change into trunks matching their pants or a two-piece matching their top. They climb the ladder, push off from the rim, and doggy-paddle. Ripples follow the landing and each stroke.
- Choose **Clear orders** while the swimmer is climbing up and they climb back down. Once they've pushed off, they finish landing before climbing out. Their usual clothes go back on when they're out of the pool.
- Saving during a swim preserves the swimmer's place in the action, including a climb out you've already requested.

## Improved

- Build highlights unreachable ground before you confirm a layout. Choose **Confirm** to keep it or **Return to build mode** to revise it. You can move or remove the original house fittings, including its doors.
- Floor finishes load separately from the rest of the scene. You can keep choosing finishes while another material loads. Placement feedback now appears beside the work.
- With nobody selected, the Sim dock shows empty outlines for mood and needs. Use the small chevron to collapse or expand the dock.
- At work, Hunger, Bladder and Social refill halfway when they would become Critical. A housemate arriving with any of those needs already Low or Critical gets the same relief.
- Each need held at zero now costs 6 mood points per game hour, up from 5. Crossing into Critical causes no immediate mood drop.
- Baths fill with water, and the bather plays with a rubber duck, toy boat or submarine. Saving during a bath keeps the toy and its place in the animation.
- Import save can open older household files that the file picker previously rejected.
