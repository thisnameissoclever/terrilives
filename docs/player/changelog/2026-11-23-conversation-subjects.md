# Conversation subjects

Housemates now chat about trains, bicycling, gardening, the weather and work, as well as the activities they enjoy.

## New

- A chat can now be about trains, bicycling, gardening, the weather or, for a housemate with a job, work. Each housemate likes some subjects more than others, and brings up the ones they like.
- How a housemate feels about each subject comes from who they are: someone who enjoys exercise leans toward bicycling, someone who likes plants toward gardening, and someone happy in their job toward work. You cannot change these feelings.
- Sim details, Overview, Likes and dislikes now lists each subject after the object rows, from Loves to Hates. Work appears only for a housemate with a job.
- A subject chat shows the Talk symbol in the usual like or dislike color, and the Log names the subject, as in a chat about the weather.
- A listener reacts to a subject by their own feelings, so one housemate can love talking about trains while the other dislikes hearing about them. A listener who dislikes the subject thinks a little less of the speaker, as with any disliked topic.
