# Higher wages, household bills, sleeping housemates and finished arguments

Every job pays half as much again and night shifts pay extra, the household pays monthly bills shown in a new Bills row and panel, a new game starts with money for the first bills, and beds belong indoors. Housemates no longer wait beside someone asleep to chat with them or argue with them, and an argument the game started now runs to its end once under way and no longer makes anyone late for work.

## New

- Every job now pays half as much again, and a night shift pays 15% more than the job's usual pay. The Career tab shows a night worker's pay with the extra.
- The household now pays four bills on the first day of each month: rent, electricity, water and transportation. A month is seven days. Rent depends on how much of the lot is indoors and how much is yard. Showers, baths, hand washing, flushing, dish washing, cooking on the stove, television, the radio, fridges, lamps, aquariums and pools add to electricity and water, and each trip to work adds a fare.
- A new game now starts with $400, enough for the first month's bills.
- Bills are paid from Funds automatically and in order, oldest first: each waits until Funds cover it and every bill before it. A bill still unpaid five days after it arrives is overdue: it gains a late fee each day, up to half the bill again, and every housemate feels **Behind on bills** until it is paid. Nothing is ever shut off or taken away.
- A new **Bills** row below Funds shows what to keep aside and when it is due, what is unpaid and its due day, or what is overdue. Select it to open the Bills panel, which pauses the game and shows each bill so far, what it is expected to come to, last month's bills and how bills work.
- Beds can only go inside a room. The game refuses to buy or move a bed onto the yard. In Build mode, Confirm is refused while a wall or room change would leave a bed outside: move the bed into a room or choose Cancel.

## Improved

- Older saved games raise their wages to the new pay. A bed already standing outdoors in an older save stays where it is and still works.

## Fixed

- Housemates no longer stand for hours beside someone asleep, waiting to chat. Nobody starts a chat of their own with a sleeping housemate, and someone already waiting for a chat gives up when the other person falls asleep and does something else; they may ask again once the sleeper wakes. A chat you order still waits until the sleeper wakes up.
- Housemates no longer start an argument with someone who is asleep, and an argument the game started gives up when the other person falls asleep before it begins. The grudge can still lead to an argument after they wake. A Fight you order still waits until the sleeper wakes up.
- An argument the game started no longer stops partway through when either housemate turns critically hungry, tired or desperate for the bathroom. It runs to its end, and both of them get Had a fight. If a need turns critical before the argument starts, the game still calls it off. An argument also ends if the person on the receiving end leaves for work.
- A housemate no longer leaves late for work because of an argument the game had lined up for them. When their shift is due, an argument that has not started is called off and they leave on time; the grudge can still lead to an argument later. An argument already under way finishes first. A Fight you order still keeps them home until it is done, as your other orders do.
