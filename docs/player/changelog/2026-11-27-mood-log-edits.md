# Mood, the Log, and housemate edits

Housemates' needs affect their mood more gently. The Log explains significant mood changes, and you can save housemate edits from any tab.

## Improved

- Empty needs lower mood more slowly. Six needs at 80% or higher offset most of the mood loss from one empty need.
- Turn on Details + mood in the Log to see significant mood changes and their causes. A continuous change, such as nearby company moving away, appears as one entry.
- Choose Save from any Edit housemate tab to apply edits from the whole draft.

## Fixed

- Housemates skip casual chats with anyone who is asleep, at work, or commuting. If someone leaves for work or falls asleep during a wait, the other housemate can get on with their own needs.
