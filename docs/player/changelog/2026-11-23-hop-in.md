# Swimming, bath toys and breaks at work

Choose **Hop in** from a pool's object menu. Housemates can also decide to swim on their own.

## New

- Swimmers change into trunks matching their pants or a two-piece matching their top. They climb the ladder, push off from the rim, and doggy-paddle. Ripples follow the landing and each stroke.
- Choose **Clear orders** while the swimmer is climbing up and they climb back down. Once they've pushed off, they finish landing before climbing out. Their usual clothes go back on when they're out of the pool.
- Saving during a swim preserves the swimmer's place in the action, including a climb out you've already requested.

## Improved

- Build highlights unreachable ground before you confirm a layout. Choose **Confirm** to keep it or **Return to build mode** to revise it. You can move or remove the original house fittings, including its doors.
- Floor finishes load separately from the rest of the scene. You can keep choosing finishes while another material loads. Placement feedback now appears beside the work.
- With nobody selected, the Sim dock shows empty outlines for mood and needs. Use the small chevron to collapse or expand the dock.
- At work, Hunger, Bladder and Social refill halfway when they would become Critical. A housemate arriving with any of those needs already Low or Critical gets the same relief.
- Each need held at zero now costs 6 mood points per game hour, up from 5. Crossing into Critical causes no immediate mood drop.
- Baths fill with water, and the bather plays with a rubber duck, toy boat or submarine. Saving during a bath keeps the toy and its place in the animation.
