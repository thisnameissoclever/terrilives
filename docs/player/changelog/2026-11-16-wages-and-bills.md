# Higher wages, household bills, slower needs, full bathroom visits, calmer furniture and late starts for urgent needs

Every job pays half as much again and night shifts pay extra, the household pays monthly bills shown in a new Bills row and panel, a new game starts with money for the first bills, beds belong indoors, every need runs down more slowly, a toilet, shower or bath runs until the meter is full, standing near furniture a housemate dislikes no longer lowers their mood, the lift from furniture they like now fades slowly after they leave, and a housemate who is starving or exhausted when a shift starts now sorts that out first.

## New

- Every job now pays half as much again, and a night shift pays 15% more than the job's usual pay. The Career tab shows a night worker's pay with the extra.
- The household now pays four bills on the first day of each month: rent, electricity, water and transportation. A month is seven days. Rent depends on how much of the lot is indoors and how much is yard. Showers, baths, hand washing, flushing, dish washing, cooking on the stove, television, the radio, fridges, lamps, aquariums and pools add to electricity and water, and each trip to work adds a fare.
- A new game now starts with $400, enough for the first month's bills.
- Bills are paid from Funds automatically and in order, oldest first: each waits until Funds cover it and every bill before it. A bill still unpaid five days after it arrives is overdue: it gains a late fee each day, up to half the bill again, and every housemate feels **Behind on bills** until it is paid. Nothing is ever shut off or taken away.
- A new **Bills** row below Funds shows what to keep aside and when it is due, what is unpaid and its due day, or what is overdue. Select it to open the Bills panel, which pauses the game and shows each bill so far, what it is expected to come to, last month's bills and how bills work.
- Beds can only go inside a room. The game refuses to buy or move a bed onto the yard. In Build mode, Confirm is refused while a wall or room change would leave a bed outside: move the bed into a room or choose Cancel.

## Improved

- A housemate who is critically hungry or exhausted when their shift starts now deals with it first and leaves for work late, at most one game hour after the shift starts. While they are late they almost always spend the time on that need. A meal or sleep already under way finishes first. Your orders still come first, needing the toilet never makes anyone late for work (housemates take care of it at work), and lateness still costs pay and, past two hours of missed work, performance.
- A housemate at work is now relieved of their Bladder whenever it falls below half, so it no longer becomes critical during a shift.
- Older saved games raise their wages to the new pay. A bed already standing outdoors in an older save stays where it is and still works.
- Housemates who dislike or hate plants or the aquarium no longer get "Bothered by the plants here" or "Bothered by the aquarium here" when they share a room with one. They simply read nothing from it. Housemates who like those things still get "Likes the plants here" or "Likes the aquarium here" while they are in the room.
- Someone who hates the television or the radio is still bothered when another housemate uses it in the same room. That mood and its effect on how they feel about the user are unchanged.
- Hunger, Energy, Hygiene, Bladder, Social, Fun and Comfort all fall about 25 percent more slowly. A full meter lasts about a third longer.
- A visit to the toilet, shower or bath now lasts until the meter it fills is full: Bladder for the toilet, Hygiene for the shower and bath. A housemate who gets very little from it can reach a time limit first and leave with the meter not full.
- If a visit stops early, for example because you give another order, the housemate keeps only the gain from the minutes they spent.
- A visit is shorter when the meter is not empty when it starts. A shower from very dirty takes about 40 minutes of game time.
- A shower costs Energy and a bath gives Comfort for every minute you stay, so a longer visit costs or gives more.
- Washing hands is unchanged.
- The lift a housemate gets from plants or the aquarium they like no longer ends the moment they leave the room. It fades slowly: one plant's lift is gone within about two game hours after they leave, and the biggest lift, from several plants, within about four. While it fades their mood list reads **Enjoyed the plants** or **Enjoyed the aquarium**, and going back to things they like lifts their mood up to what the room gives, but no higher. Saved games from before this start with no lift fading.
