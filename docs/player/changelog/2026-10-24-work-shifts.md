# Work shifts and five new careers

Jobs now come with shifts, there are five new careers, you can choose or change a housemate's shift in the Career tab, and putting a book back no longer holds up the start of work.

## New

- Five new careers: Hospital, Warehouse, Library, Restaurant kitchen and Buses, each with four levels.
- Jobs now come with shifts. Depending on the career, a job can be a day, early, late or night shift, and a new hire is given one by chance. Office work is nearly always a weekday day shift. Housemates leave for work and come home at their own shift's times.
- The Career tab in Sim details names each housemate's shift. When you assign a career you can pick a shift or leave it to chance. You can also change an employed housemate's shift while they are at home: their old shift is still worked that day if it has not started yet, and changing shift resets the good weeks counted toward their next promotion.
- Nobody works two shifts in one day. A new shift or a newly assigned job starts on a day after the day the housemate's last shift ended, so after a night shift that ends in the morning it can be two days after the change.
- Night workers sleep during the day. Early workers go to bed about an hour earlier, and late workers about two hours later.

## Improved

- Housemates looking for work now try different careers by chance, still favoring the ones that suit their traits, rather than always picking the same best fit.
- Older saved games keep every job on its usual hours.

## Fixed

- A housemate putting a book back no longer stands waiting while someone else walks over to the same bookcase for a book, which could make them more than two hours late for work. Only the person taking or shelving a book holds the spot in front of the bookcase, and anyone who arrives meanwhile waits there only for the people ahead of them to finish.
