# Work shifts, higher wages and household bills

Jobs now come with shifts and pay half as much again, there are five new careers, the household pays monthly bills, beds belong indoors, and putting a book back no longer holds up the start of work.

## New

- Five new careers: Hospital, Warehouse, Library, Restaurant kitchen and Buses, each with four levels.
- Jobs now come with shifts. Depending on the career, a job can be a day, early, late or night shift, and a new hire is given one by chance. Office work is nearly always a weekday day shift. Housemates leave for work and come home at their own shift's times.
- The Career tab in Sim details names each housemate's shift. When you assign a career you can pick a shift or leave it to chance. You can also change an employed housemate's shift while they are at home: their old shift is still worked that day if it has not started yet, and changing shift resets the good weeks counted toward their next promotion.
- Nobody works two shifts in one day. A new shift or a newly assigned job starts on a day after the day the housemate's last shift ended, so after a night shift that ends in the morning it can be two days after the change.
- Night workers sleep during the day. Early workers go to bed about an hour earlier, and late workers about two hours later.
- Every job now pays half as much again, and a night shift pays 15% more than the job's usual pay. The Career tab shows a night worker's pay with the extra.
- The household now pays four bills on the first day of each month: rent, electricity, water and transportation. A month is seven days. Rent depends on how much of the lot is indoors and how much is yard. Showers, baths, hand washing, flushing, dish washing, cooking on the stove, television, the radio, fridges, lamps, aquariums and pools add to electricity and water, and each trip to work adds a fare.
- A new game now starts with $400, enough for the first month's bills.
- Bills are paid from Funds automatically and in order, oldest first: each waits until Funds cover it and every bill before it. A bill still unpaid five days after it arrives is overdue: it gains a late fee each day, up to half the bill again, and every housemate feels **Behind on bills** until it is paid. Nothing is ever shut off or taken away.
- A new **Bills** row below Funds shows what to keep aside and when it is due, or what is overdue. Select it to open the Bills panel, which pauses the game and shows each bill so far, what it is expected to come to, last month's bills and how bills work.
- Beds can only go inside a room. The game refuses to buy or move a bed onto the yard. In Build mode, Confirm is refused while a wall or room change would leave a bed outside: move the bed into a room or choose Cancel.

## Improved

- Housemates looking for work now try different careers by chance, still favoring the ones that suit their traits, rather than always picking the same best fit.
- Older saved games keep every job on its usual hours, and their wages rise to the new pay. A bed already standing outdoors in an older save stays where it is and still works.

## Fixed

- A housemate putting a book back no longer stands waiting while someone else walks over to the same bookcase for a book, which could make them more than two hours late for work. Only the person taking or shelving a book holds the spot in front of the bookcase, and anyone who arrives meanwhile waits there only for the people ahead of them to finish.
