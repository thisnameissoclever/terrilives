# Larger pools, conversation memory, chores and hobbies

Housemates can share the larger pools, remember each other's favorite topics and feel better while doing their hobbies. People without jobs are more likely to take on chores. Object and person menus open beside their targets.

## New

- Housemates learn what each person likes and dislikes talking about, and steer their chats toward the topics the other person enjoys and away from ones they dislike.
- A more sociable housemate remembers more of their chats and makes better guesses about any topic they do not remember.
- Memories fade over about six game months unless another chat about the same topic brings them back. Games saved before this update start with nothing remembered.
- Backyard Lodge is a 3×4 above-ground pool with wood sides and exterior stairs.
- Garden Basin is a 5×7 in-ground pool with a ladder inside the short end. Pools already placed in saved games keep their original footprints.

## Improved

- Doing a favorite activity, such as reading, gradually lifts a housemate's mood while they're doing it. Saving mid-activity keeps the lift when they resume.
- The chore board is more likely to assign duties to housemates without jobs.
- Housemates without jobs are more likely to do chores on their own, especially when there's nobody around to talk to and their needs can wait.
- Backyard Lodge fits two swimmers, and Garden Basin fits four. Swimmers take turns on the ladder.
- Housemates change into swimwear and recline on a Sunstrip lounger with their legs extended.
- Existing shared swimming saves and saves from the hobby preview keep their progress. The game makes a recovery copy before replacing either kind of save stored in your browser.
- Right-click or long-press an object or person to open a compact list beside it. Scroll the menu on smaller screens to reach every action.
- Bookcase actions show which book you will buy or sell and what it costs or pays.

## Fixed

- Buying land while a housemate is swimming keeps their swim in progress.
- A housemate can reach the Sunstrip lounger from either long side. Sit reports the lounger as unreachable when both sides are blocked.
- The game keeps running smoothly with a furniture menu open, including at 3× speed.
