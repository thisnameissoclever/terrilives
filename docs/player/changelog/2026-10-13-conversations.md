# Conversation topics and company

Sims have more reasons to spend time together, even when they do not get along.

## New

- Conversations have activity topics. Sims who enjoy socializing are more likely to choose a shared interest. Each person's bubble shows the topic and whether they like it.
- Family provides a small extra Social benefit, even between relatives who dislike each other.

## Improved

- Talking and shared activities restore Social even with disliked company. Liked company still provides more benefit.
- Sims can approach a busy conversation partner and wait nearby until their activity finishes. Available partners wait for the approaching Sim.
- Joining a shared activity can extend the other participants' time together, up to twice per person during that activity.
- Saves preserve conversation topics, waiting partners and shared-activity extensions. Older saves remain loadable, and the first new save preserves the older file as a recovery copy.
