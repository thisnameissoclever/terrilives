# Quick help

## Build & place

1. Build pauses the household. Select furniture and a destination, turn it with the curved arrows, then Place. Cancel beside a selection, or Escape, clears that selection and keeps your other changes; with nothing selected, Escape leaves Build mode. Use Confirm at the bottom to keep this batch and continue building. Cancel there discards every change since the last Confirm and keeps Build open. Exit build or Escape with changes that are not confirmed asks first: Confirm changes, Discard changes or Keep building.

2. Walls selects the edge between two floor tiles. Choose Wall, Doorway, Window or Remove beside the edge. Rotation turns the selection without changing the house. A window stops people and lets daylight through. The status explains refused edits.

3. Rooms opens Select rooms. Click a floor or choose a room from the dropdown, then edit its name. Names stay pending across tool changes; Enter in the name field does not confirm. Outside counts as one room. Boundaries update when you Confirm. Splits, merges and lost enclosures open a review; Back to build keeps your edits. Build room mode retains rectangle construction: choose two opposite corners, choose a doorway if needed, then Build room. Confirm keeps the batch and leaves Build open.

4. Buy lists everything for sale with its price. Choose something, select its destination, use the curved arrows to turn it, and press Buy. The price comes out of Funds. Unaffordable items are disabled.

5. Floors selects a tile without changing it. Choose Boards, Tiles, Carpet or Outdoor patio beside the tile to apply a covering. Remove restores its original floor in the draft. Confirm at the bottom keeps changes.

## People & actions

1. Select a name at the bottom of the screen. On phones, open Sim details to see their needs.

2. Tap or click an object to give the selected person its default action.

3. Long-press or right-click for more actions, including talking to another person.

4. Queue mode starts on: new actions go after the waiting ones. Turn it off to put a new action first, keeping the waiting actions after it. Holding Ctrl or Cmd also adds an action after the waiting ones.

5. Use Clear orders to stop the action you gave the selected person and remove their waiting actions.

6. The dock shows mood and life satisfaction. Life satisfaction ranges from 0 to 100 and changes gradually with sustained mood and completed activities. Hover or focus its meter for the exact score. Open Sim details for needs, mood reasons and career. Choose Traits to see their traits; Close returns to the compact dock.

7. Choose People inside Sim details to see how the selected person feels about everyone else.

## Company & chores

1. [Chat](relationships/#conversations) supplies Social and Fun while people talk. Shared interests can improve feelings. Family and partners gain more goodwill and lose less from bad interactions. [Handle correspondence](activities/#other-pastimes) at a desk supplies Social while alone.

2. [Autonomous arguments](relationships/#fights) need a real grievance: a privacy violation, an overdue assigned chore, a nearby hated activity or a chat that interrupted an unhelped need. Bad mood makes them more likely; good mood and liking the other person make them less likely. Urgent Hunger, Energy and Bladder take priority. Fights supply no Social.

3. After a completed chore argument, the assigned person may choose to start the work. Cleanliness preference, responsibility, commitment, chore preference, feelings and mood affect that decision. Completing the same duty within four game hours can [repair feelings](relationships/#chore-repair) with the initiator and housemates who complained. Completed cleaning also earns appreciation without an argument, even with the chore board off. Housemates with a higher cleanliness preference appreciate it more.

4. [Fresh mess](relationships/#mess-and-nuisance) gets time before ordinary mood penalties and complaints: two game hours continuously at maximum grime, or a full game day unfinished. Dishes blocking a seat cause an immediate problem. Cleaning remains available during the grace period. Open [Chores](ui-elements/#chores) to inspect assignments or send the selected person with Do now.

## Camera

1. Drag to move the camera. Pinch on touch or use the mouse wheel on desktop to zoom. Build mode also shows minus and plus zoom buttons.

## Options & saves

1. Open Options for Light, Sound, Death, Save, Load, New game, New housemate, Help and Changelog. Changelog opens the game's update history in a new tab. Open Sim details, then Queue, for Queue mode and Clear orders. Light: flat gives neutral daylight without the automatic lighting cycle; Sound mutes everything; Effects sets all sound volume; Voices adjusts conversations within that mix.
