# Start here

Natural Causes is a household life sim. Your housemates choose activities for themselves, develop their own feelings about one another, and still need dinner when you have other plans. You can let them get on with life, give individual orders, change their home, or add a new housemate.

## Your first few minutes {#first-steps}

1. Choose a name in the Household row at the bottom of the game to select that person. On desktop, their needs appear in the dock. On a phone, open Sim details to see the needs in Overview.
2. Pause with the speed controls while you look around. Drag the house to move the camera; use the zoom buttons, mouse wheel or a pinch to change the view.
3. Find a low [need](needs/#overview). Hunger needs food, Energy needs sleep, Hygiene needs bathing, and Bladder needs a toilet. Click or tap a suitable object to give its default action. Right-click or long-press it to choose a particular action.
4. Open [Queue](ui-elements/#activity-and-orders) to see your instructions. Queue mode starts on, so a new order joins the end. Turn it off when the next action should start first.
5. Resume and watch the whole activity. Walking to a fridge and cooking do not relieve Hunger; eating the food does. Other benefits often arrive during use.
6. Open Options, then Save when you want to keep this household. The game also autosaves at the start of a new game day. Load and New game ask before replacing progress.

## When someone needs help {#help-a-housemate}

| What you see | What to try | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hungry | Fridge: Grab a snack or Cook dinner; dining table: Eat prepared food when a portion exists | Hunger rises after the eating step finishes. Cooking alone pays nothing. |
| Tired | Bed: Sleep or Go to bed | Sleep restores Energy. Reclining gives only a small amount. |
| Dirty or smelly | Take a shower or Take a bath | Washing hands has a partial-cleanliness ceiling and cannot remove body odor. |
| Needs the toilet | Use the toilet | Bathing and hand washing do not relieve Bladder. |
| Lonely | Chat, Handle correspondence, or arrange eligible shared use | Solo correspondence supplies Social. Solo TV and standing near someone do not. |
| Bored | Read, watch TV, listen to the radio or use another enjoyable activity | Sitting by itself supplies Comfort rather than Fun. |
| Uncomfortable | Sit, lie down, sleep or bathe | Furniture has different benefits; a real occupied chair also helps during meals and media use. |
| Waiting | Check the intended object, route and [queue](home-and-controls/#orders) | Another person can hold the object, chair or activity destination. |

## Letting people choose {#autonomy}

Without your orders, housemates weigh their needs, preferences, available furniture and travel time. They do not follow a fixed checklist. Personality, hobbies, repetition and sleep habits help explain why two people make different choices.

Critical needs steer their own choices toward useful relief. Your explicit orders retain priority even when needs become critical. Need loss continues while they carry out your instructions, and prolonged empty Hunger or Energy can cause [death](the-world/#death) when that option is enabled. Cancel or redirect an unsafe queue yourself.

## Three different kinds of wellbeing {#wellbeing}

Needs describe immediate bodily and social requirements. [Mood](mood-and-personality/#mood) describes how someone feels now, including needs, conditions, surroundings and company. [Life satisfaction](mood-and-personality/#life-satisfaction) is their longer-term assessment. A clean floor can improve mood without refilling Hygiene; a friend nearby can comfort someone without refilling Social.

## Find an answer {#find-an-answer}

Use the guide search for an action, object or symptom. Try washing hands, lonely, angry, meals or Queue. Open Numbers and limits within an article when you want the detailed reference. The same disclosure works by click, tap or keyboard.

Browse [Needs](needs/#overview), [Activities](activities/#all-actions), [Relationships](relationships/#directional-feelings), [Home and controls](home-and-controls/#orders), or [The world](the-world/#calendar) when you want a fuller explanation. The [Glossary](glossary/#terms) defines the game's terms.

## If the game cannot load {#loading-failure}

Choose Copy debug details on the failure screen to copy the retained report for Tim. If copying is blocked, select and copy the displayed text instead. Email Tim opens your mail application with the game version in the subject, the recorded error and the newest logs that fit. Review the draft before sending it. Copy and download retain more detail than the email excerpt.

If the browser replaces the game with its own error page, open [Startup report](https://thisnameissoclever.github.io/terrilives/startup-report.html) in that same browser. The report page opens without starting the game and provides the same sharing controls. Download report saves a file if you prefer. The report contains loading steps and error details, with no household save.

If the game is still open, choose [Options, then Report bug](ui-elements/#options) to prepare the same email with the latest recorded details. You can describe the problem in the draft before sending it.
