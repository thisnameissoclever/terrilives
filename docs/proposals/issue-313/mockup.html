Start here

⌄

Select a person using the name buttons at the bottom of the screen. Open Sim details to check their needs. To add another person, open Options and choose New housemate.

  1. Find a person's needs, mood and other details.
  2. Add a new housemate.
  3. Find activities that improve Social.
  4. See what improves or harms relationships.
  5. Learn about seasons, falling leaves and snow.
  6. Read about hobbies and planned creative activities.

Give someone an action #

  1. Select their name at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Right-click an object on desktop, or long-press it on a touchscreen, to open its available actions.
  3. Choose the action you want. If other orders are waiting, the new action normally joins the end of the queue. Open Queue to review the person's orders.

Use Pause if you want time to choose without the household continuing. Your orders take priority over automatic self-care, so keep an eye on the selected person's needs.

Options #

Open Options beside Build, beneath the time controls. It contains household settings, New housemate, Save, Load, New game, Help and Changelog. Use Close Options to return to the game view.

Add a new housemate #

  1. Open Options.
  2. Choose New housemate to open character creation.
  3. Choose the new person's details in the form before adding them to the household.

Edit, beside the selected person's name, changes that person. It does not add somebody new.

Needs and Sim details #

Select a name at the bottom of the screen. On desktop, the expanded bottom panel shows that person's needs, mood and life satisfaction. On phones, the bottom panel shows mood and life satisfaction; tap Sim details to see the needs. Expanding the phone panel does not move the needs into it. Sim details also contains temporary mood effects, career and personal details. Choose People inside Sim details to see how that person feels about the other housemates.

Needs describe things such as hunger or loneliness. Relationships describe one person's opinion of another. The two can change independently.

Clock and weather #

The time panel shows the day and weekday, the time, and a line such as Winter: snowing. That last line names the season and current weather. See Weather and seasons for what the weather looks like and what it affects.

Needs and activities

⌄

Each person has seven needs: Hunger, Energy, Hygiene, Bladder, Social, Fun and Comfort. Select a person to see their need meters. Activities can refill some needs while time and effort reduce others.

SocialWash hands

Social: company #

Chatting provides company while both people talk. Watching television or listening to the radio provides company only when another person actively uses the same device at the same time. A housemate merely standing nearby or walking over does not count.

To have two people watch together, select the first person and choose Watch TV on a television. Then select the second person and choose Watch TV on that same television. Both must be able to reach an available viewing position and begin watching.

Eating together provides company while both people eat real food in separate chairs facing the same table. Standing with a plate or sitting at an empty table does not provide that benefit. Finishing somebody else's cooking can also change the eater's opinion of the cook.

Reading, exercise and aquarium watching can provide a small amount of company when both people do the same kind of activity on separate objects in the same room, within four floor tiles, and the activity suits both people's preferences.

Company can improve Social even between people who dislike each other. It does not guarantee that they will get along: see relationship effects.

Wash hands #

  1. Select the person.
  2. Right-click or long-press a kitchen or bathroom sink.
  3. Choose Wash hands.

The person must reach the sink and begin washing before Hygiene improves. Washing hands provides limited cleaning; it cannot replace a bath or shower, and repeating it cannot remove body odor.

Hand-washing numbers and limits

Hand washing cannot raise Hygiene above 40 out of 100. Clearing body odor requires Hygiene above 40. Washing when already above that limit does not reduce Hygiene to 40.

The bathroom sink's baseline is 32 Hygiene over 21 game minutes; the kitchen sink's is 20 over 23 game minutes. The benefit arrives while washing. Actual time spent, personal refill rates, the ceiling and continuing need loss change the final result.

Relationships

⌄

Each person has their own opinion of every housemate. Alex can like Sam while Sam dislikes Alex. To check one person's opinions, select them and open Sim details > People.

Sharing foodConversation topicsFights

Eating a meal someone else cooked #

When a person finishes a prepared portion cooked by another living housemate, their opinion of the cook improves a little. The cook's opinion does not change from this effect. Collecting food or stopping before finishing does not grant appreciation.

This appreciation can happen while eating standing. The separate Social benefit from shared meals requires seated eating at the same table.

Conversation topics #

A listener who dislikes the conversation topic can finish with a worse opinion of the person who started the chat. Both can still receive Social while talking. Filling a need does not mean the conversation improved their relationship.

Fights #

A completed fight lowers both participants' opinions of each other and leaves both with a temporary negative mood effect. Fighting refills no need. An interrupted fight does not apply its completion penalty.

Calendar #

A new game begins in Spring. Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter last seven game days each, making a 28-day year. The current calendar has no months. Read the current season in the time panel.

Weather and seasons #

Most periods are mild. Summer can have hot periods, Autumn can have falling leaves, and Winter can have snow. Weather varies by day; a season does not guarantee a particular event.

The grass stays green with subtle seasonal color changes. Weather currently changes the scene, not people's needs or mood. With reduced motion enabled, the weather text remains but the particles are hidden.

Coming soon: weather preferences #

Sims will have preferences for warmer or cooler weather. Weather they enjoy will slightly improve their mood; weather they dislike will slightly lower it. More variation from month to month and year to year is also planned. These features are not available yet, and no release date has been announced.

Mood and personality: hobbies

⌄

Hobbies describe activities a person enjoys and can contribute to life satisfaction. Skills describe learned ability. Enjoying an activity and being good at it are different things.

Coming soon: making things through hobbies #

Gardening, knitting, painting and other creative hobbies are planned. Sims will gain personal satisfaction as they develop these hobbies and level up, even when they are doing something simply because they enjoy it.

They will eventually make things for their homes, such as paintings, flowers and throw blankets. Some creations may be sold for money. Gardening may also provide food for the fridge and reduce food spending.

These features are not available yet. Exact level requirements, sale prices and food yields have not been decided, and no release date has been announced.
