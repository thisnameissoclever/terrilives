samples on 2026-09-17. Independent subjective correctness: 88/100.

The raised lid clears the cistern and both ceramic support sections. Its lower
edge and hinge remain visible in the front views; the rear views have coherent
tank occlusion. The palette and smooth contours match the accepted bathroom
sink and kitchen sources.

Nonblocking limitations: broad shading makes the ceramic deck beneath the
hinge less distinct from the rear inner rim. The stepped support behind the
bowl is boxier than the front shell. Neither reviewer found a mechanical
impossibility in this candidate.

All four image hashes, model hash and eight source hashes were independently
verified. `scene-check.json` records positive cistern clearance, bowl depth,
open seat, supported fittings and eight caught in-memory mutations. Five
inside-solid contact witnesses establish contact at the lid/axle, both
mounts/axle and both mounts/bowl. The independent reviewer inspected that
report and checker but did not rerun Blender.

This accepts the static source only. Runtime placement, occupied fit and
publication require separate evidence. It does not add sitting, clothing,
flushing or lid-animation behavior. Retain all originals and earlier rejected
candidates unchanged.
