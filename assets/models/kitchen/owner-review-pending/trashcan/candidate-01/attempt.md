# Trash can candidate 01

Method: authored Blender geometry through `render_trashcan.py`. No image-model
request or paid generation. The accepted Sim supplies the camera, lights and
toon material family. Exact inputs, model and four original PNG hashes are
recorded in `proof.json`.

Design brief: a compact closed grey pedal bin, below counter height, with a
grounded base, fitted lid and restrained dark hardware. Preserve the one-tile
scenery role, placement, price and lack of interactions.

Primary and independent source review accepted all four 768x960 RGBA originals
and the reduced review board. Independent subjective score: 93/100, not a
calibrated measurement. The rear views partly hide the pedal as expected.
No source revision was requested and no rejected output exists in this batch.

`surface-contact-proof.json` verifies five closed connected solids, a grounded
base and five evaluated-surface contacts. Eight damaged copies and three deleted
guards are rejected without changing the saved model bytes.

Acceptance covers the closed exterior. It does not establish a hollow interior,
working pedal mechanism, opening animation, owner approval or public deployment.
