# Stove candidate 01

Superseded on 2026-09-17. Retained for comparison, not selected for integration.

The four views preserve the blue-grey enamel, cream hob and brass details of
the existing kitchen. Primary review found coherent rotation and silhouette.
Independent review rated the closed static appearance 85/100, with no blocking
style or occlusion defect, but found small mechanical and contour defects.

1. Each knob indicator sat 0.0185 model units in front of its knob face.
   A small detail at game scale still needs to be physically attached.
2. Burner-well rims had small tooth-like contour marks at source resolution.
   These were not evident in the 192x240 texture samples. Smooth the cylinder
   sides without changing the flat caps.
3. Follow-up geometry inspection found a 0.007-unit gap under the coil rings.
   Lower the coils into contact with their supporting wells.

Candidate 02 will address these measurements. Keep all four originals, the
editable scene and proof unchanged. Rear burners may be occluded by the rear
upstand in back views; that is correct depth, not missing geometry.
