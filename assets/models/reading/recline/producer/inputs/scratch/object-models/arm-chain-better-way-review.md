## What was taken as fixed but is not

[F1] Zero shoulder roll, exact side-grip orientation and the chosen elbow circle point are probe choices. The upstream shoulder rotation is available, but its safe range depends on actual skin and sleeve deformation. [F2] The resting actors need credible resting gestures. Their current direct local rotations deliberately suspend the hands and have no contact target (`seated_pose_mixed_v2.py:119-134`). [P1] Analytic construction detail: let `a` and `b` be normalized source upper/forearm directions, `u` the target upper direction, and `L` the retained forearm deformation rotation. Find quaternion `q0` mapping `a` to `L.T @ u = w`. Every candidate `q = spin(w,t) * q0` still maps that upper direction correctly. Require `dot(vector(q), b) = 0`, so the relative elbow rotation axis is perpendicular to the source forearm. This is one sine/cosine equation in `t/2`, not an optimizer. With `A = dot(vector(q0), b)` and `B = dot(scalar(q0)*w + cross(w, vector(q0)), b)`, choose `t/2 = atan2(-A, B)` and set `U = L * rotation(q)`. Check the reconstructed shortest elbow transport and endpoint residuals explicitly. Reject antiparallel or underdetermined inputs instead of silently choosing a world axis. The numerical result in [D4], complete-chain feasibility, used this construction.
